# Scope map

## Literal subject boundary

- Finite-rank complex vector bundles, or a source-justified equivalent classifying-space model.
- Integral graded cohomology as the conventional codomain of component classes `c_i`.
- A component family and, where selected, its total-class packaging.
- Existence, uniqueness, pullback naturality, Whitney-sum behavior, normalization, and rank
  vanishing are candidate theorem components. The inventory phrase selects none of them.

The local wording is a topic label rather than a proposition. A definition of assumed class data,
a construction theorem, an axiomatic characterization, a single formal identity, and a
Chern-Weil comparison result have different domains, hypotheses, and conclusions.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must select and inspect one exact source theorem. It must fix topological,
smooth, or algebraic bundle context; admissible base spaces; finite rank; reduced or unreduced
cohomology; integral coefficient representation; degree conventions; whether `c_0 = 1` and
`c_i = 0` above the rank are conclusions or definitional behavior; the normalization on line
bundles; pullback naturality; the exact component or total Whitney sum formula; and all ordered
binders, universes, and foundation assumptions.

## Explicit exclusions

- Stiefel-Whitney, Pontryagin, Euler, or generic characteristic classes as substitutes.
- Chern characters, Chern numbers, or classifications by Chern classes unless source-selected.
- Assuming a structure whose fields already contain all requested laws and proving a projection.
- Chern-Weil representatives without the additional manifold, connection, curvature, and
  de Rham-to-integral comparison data.
- The generated `已验证` label as human-source or kernel evidence.

No canonical Lean declaration is selected at intake. If source inspection does not determine one
exact proposition, the statement phase must remain blocked.
