# Scope map

## Preserved theorem family

The intake preserves exactly the repository phrase: a norm inequality for the Fourier transform
under the name Hausdorff-Young. The conventional family relates an input exponent between the
`L1` and `L2` endpoints to a conjugate output exponent, but that observation is a source-selection
lead, not the frozen proposition.

The statement phase must choose one source formulation rather than combining these materially
different surfaces:

- Fourier coefficients of periodic functions or functions on the circle;
- the Fourier transform on `R`, `R^n`, or a finite-dimensional real inner-product space;
- Fourier analysis on a compact, discrete, finite, or general locally compact abelian group; and
- an unsharp contractive inequality versus a normalization-dependent or sharp-constant theorem.

## Required statement decisions

1. Select and preserve an immutable primary or authoritative source edition, exact result/page,
   incorporated definitions, proof boundary, and correction/translation status.
2. Fix the domain and dual domain, topology, measurable structures, Haar or volume measures, and
   all universe and typeclass data.
3. Fix the Fourier kernel, sign, `2*pi` convention, and measure normalization on both sides.
4. Fix real- or complex-valued functions, representatives modulo almost-everywhere equality,
   strong measurability, and whether the transform begins on Schwartz, `L1 cap Lp`, or `Lp` and is
   extended by density.
5. Fix the ordered binders for the exponent `p`, conjugate exponent `q`, their encoding and range,
   endpoint inclusion, and the treatment of `p = 1`, `p = 2`, `q = infinity`, and any zero
   exponent excluded by the source.
6. Fix the exact conclusion: existence/membership of the transformed object, a seminorm or norm
   inequality, the constant and its parameter dependence, and whether equality or optimality is
   included.
7. Relate alternate Fourier-series, Euclidean, and LCA-group encodings only through separately
   sourced and kernel-checked transports. Freeze mutation tests before proof search.

## Boundary and degenerate cases

No case is excluded at intake. Source review must explicitly dispose of the zero function, the
zero-dimensional Euclidean space, trivial or finite groups, zero/infinite Haar measure where
applicable, null modifications, nonintegrable representatives, both exponent endpoints, and any
normalization for which the norm-one inequality changes constant.

## Exclusions

- `THM-M-0295` cannot be merged into or substituted for this target by a worker.
- The `L1 -> L-infinity` estimate alone is only one endpoint, not the complete usual
  Hausdorff-Young interval.
- The `L2` Plancherel norm equality alone is a neighboring endpoint theorem, not the requested
  inequality family.
- Young's scalar inequality, Young's convolution inequality, Riemann-Lebesgue, Fourier inversion,
  and Plancherel are not name-based substitutes.
- A theorem with an assumed bounded transform, packaged norm inequality, or oracle field would be
  circular.
- The catalog's `已验证` label and this intake's API checks provide no proof credit.

The later formal statement must provide a concrete transform between concrete source and target
spaces or record a precise API blocker. Its source choice must also explain why this target remains
distinct from `THM-M-0295` or send that duplication question to the integration lane without
altering either target.
