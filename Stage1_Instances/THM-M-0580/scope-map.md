# Scope map

## Included root

The intended root is the three-dimensional Poincare conjecture: under conventions to be frozen,
a closed, connected, simply connected topological 3-manifold is homeomorphic to `S^3`. The later
statement phase must decide whether orientability is explicit or derived, how boundarylessness and
dimension are represented, and whether connectedness is redundant under the selected definition.

## Source and mathematical surface

- Perelman's Ricci flow with surgery, no-local-collapsing, finite-extinction, and geometrization
  machinery insofar as it supplies the Poincare corollary.
- The translation from smooth Ricci-flow hypotheses/conclusions to the topological manifold root.
- The exact category bridges among smooth, PL, and topological 3-manifolds.
- Degenerate checks: empty types, disconnected spaces, manifolds with boundary, noncompact spaces,
  non-Hausdorff conventions, and homotopy-sphere versus merely simply-connected formulations.

## Explicit exclusions

- Perelman entropy, reduced volume, or any isolated Ricci-flow lemma as a substitute for the root.
- The full geometrization conjecture as the canonical root; it may be a stronger source bridge only.
- The four-dimensional or generalized Poincare conjecture.
- A proposition assuming the desired homeomorphism, or an abstract predicate filled by a hypothesis.
- Legacy metadata or partial topology wrappers as proof credit.

The statement phase must freeze universes, all typeclasses and binders, the sphere model, precise
homeomorphism conclusion, imports, environment fingerprint, alternate encodings, and hypothesis
mutation tests.
