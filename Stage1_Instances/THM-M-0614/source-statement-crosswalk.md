  Letters* **1** (1994), 809-822. This is a primary anchor for the symplectic nonvanishing result
  and the start of the comparison program; it must not automatically be cited as the complete
  equality asserted by the metadata gloss.
- Clifford H. Taubes, "The Seiberg-Witten and Gromov invariants", *Mathematical Research Letters*
  **2** (1995), 221-238. This is the closest primary discovery anchor for the named comparison.
  An exact theorem number/page and all incorporated definitions must still be checked from a stable
  edition.
- Clifford H. Taubes, "SW => Gr: From the Seiberg-Witten equations to pseudo-holomorphic curves",
  *Journal of the American Mathematical Society* **9** (1996), 845-918. This is a primary anchor
  for one direction and its analytic construction, not by itself permission to claim the converse
  or the final convention-level equality.

These are discovery anchors only. This intake has not pinned immutable source copies, checked
corrections across the series, produced a theorem/page/assumption crosswalk, or obtained independent
review. Consequently they establish neither `H0` nor machine closure.

## Required source-to-statement work

Before a canonical target can be frozen, a source reviewer must:

1. select a stable primary edition and transcribe the exact comparison statement with a pinpoint
   theorem/page anchor;
2. record the smoothness, compactness, connectedness, orientation, symplectic, `b2+`, genericity,
   and coefficient hypotheses exactly as used;
3. map the `Spin^c`, Chern-class, Poincare-duality, and homology/cohomology indexing conventions;
4. state the definitions and normalizations of both invariants, including signs, chambers,
   exceptional classes, tori, and multiple covers;
5. distinguish the two implication directions and crosswalk every additional paper needed for the
   final equality;
6. inspect corrections, later clarifications, and boundary cases; and
7. obtain independent approval of the source-to-canonical-statement mapping.

The Lean-side crosswalk must map every sourced object and hypothesis to an exact type or predicate,
classify missing gauge theory and pseudoholomorphic-curve infrastructure, and kernel-check every
credited alternate encoding. Until then, no exact Lean proposition or expression fingerprint is
