This phase freezes 13 canonical obligations before proof-node acceptance. The
machine denominator contains the exact root, statement interfaces, foundation
audit, pinned Zorn bridge, and exact assembly transport. Human-source and
provenance boundaries remain separately visible and cannot contribute machine
proof credit.

## M0770-ROOT

The root is the fingerprinted `ZornsLemmaTarget`: every nonempty partial order
whose nonempty chains are bounded above has an `IsMax` element. It requires the
exact assembly node; its present machine debt is M3.

## M0770-S-DEFINITIONS

maximality by greatestness.

## M0770-S-DOMAINS
