## Decisions required before statement freeze

The source audit must determine whether the target is a characterization of Iitaka dimension, an
addition theorem for a fibration, or another named result. It must then freeze the base field,
variety/scheme hypotheses, properness and smoothness assumptions, divisor or canonical divisor,
section-ring convention, treatment of no sections (`-infinity`), quantifiers, and equality or
inequality direction. Degenerate dimensions and reducible/non-normal cases must be explicit.
