## Proof route

The selected route maps the inverse continuous map through integral singular homology. The two
homotopy-equivalence laws produce homotopies of `f ≫ g` and `g ≫ f` to identities. The pinned
mathlib bridge turns each homotopy into equality of induced homology maps. Functoriality then gives
both inverse equations, which install `F.map g` as an inverse to `F.map f`.

`ObligationTree.lean` validates only the final child-to-parent composition: an explicit
`InducedInverseLaws` premise yields the exact canonical `HomotopyInvarianceStatement`. It does not
provide a proof-phase body for that premise. The anchor audit's exact candidate is discovery
evidence and is deliberately not credited as accepted root closure in this phase.
