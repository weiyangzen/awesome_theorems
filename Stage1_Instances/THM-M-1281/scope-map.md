# Scope map

## Provisional included claim

- A compact smooth Riemannian manifold, with the source's closedness/connectedness conventions.
- Dimension at least six (subject to verification against the selected theorem in the primary
  edition).
- Failure of local conformal flatness, expressed geometrically and, where justified, by nonzero
  Weyl curvature at some point.
- Strict inequality between its conformal Yamabe constant and the sharp constant of the round
  sphere.
- The resulting existence of a positive conformal factor giving constant scalar curvature is a
  downstream analytic consequence whose exact inclusion in the root must be decided from source.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

The source audit must freeze the edition, theorem/page, dimension range, compact/closed and
connected hypotheses, normalization of the conformal Laplacian and Yamabe functional, exponent,
volume convention, scalar-curvature sign, meaning of local conformal flatness, and whether the root
is the strict inequality, attainment/existence, or a combined statement. It must cover disconnected
and zero-dimensional cases, dimensions below six, conformally flat metrics, zero Weyl tensor, and
the distinction between a metric's conformal constant and a manifold-level smooth invariant.

## Explicit exclusions

- The full Yamabe theorem for all compact manifolds, including Schoen's conformally flat branch.
- Aubin-Lions compactness, the Aubin-Talenti sharp Sobolev inequality, or any other theorem sharing
  Aubin's name.
- A generic minimizer-existence theorem with the strict sphere inequality assumed as a hypothesis.
- A structure that contains the desired metric, minimizer, or inequality as a field.
- The repository label `已验证` as primary-source or Lean kernel evidence.
