# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

The repository fixes only the Chinese label `Julia集`, the gloss `复动力学的排斥集` ("the
repelling set of complex dynamics"), Gaston Julia, 1918, importance "high," and an untrusted
`已验证` status. It supplies no bibliography, selected map, ambient space, definition, theorem
locator, premise, conclusion, or formal artifact. The intake therefore preserves an object/topic
and historical boundary only.

## Proposition-changing decisions

An approved source correction must select one immutable primary-source proposition and freeze:

- whether the dynamics is generated by a polynomial, rational self-map of the Riemann sphere, or
  another holomorphic or meromorphic map, with coefficient field, degree, and exceptional cases;
- the ambient complex plane, extended complex plane, or Riemann-sphere encoding, including its
  topology or spherical metric and the treatment of infinity and poles;
- the exact iterate construction and its domain at critical, pole, and exceptional points;
- the Julia-set definition: non-normality of the iterate family, complement of a Fatou set,
  closure of repelling periodic points, boundary of a filled Julia set, or another source-defined
  predicate;
- all supporting definitions, including normal family, equicontinuity, Fatou set, filled Julia
  set, periodic point, minimal period, multiplier, and repelling behavior;
- whether the intended conclusion is a definition, an equivalence among definitions, invariance,
  nonemptiness, closedness, perfectness, density, connectedness, a boundary identity, or a theorem
  about one concrete map;
- the order and scope of quantifiers over maps, points, periods, neighborhoods, subsequences, and
  convergence, together with every regularity and nondegeneracy hypothesis; and
- all boundary cases, including degree below two, infinity, critical or exceptional orbits,
  period zero, and empty or whole-space sets.

These choices give inequivalent propositions. They are a resolution checklist, not a canonical
claim.

## Candidate families not credited

- Defining the Julia set as the locus where the family of iterates is not normal.
- Identifying the Julia set with the complement of a source-defined Fatou set.
- Identifying it with the closure of repelling periodic points for a degree-at-least-two rational
  map.
- Proving nonemptiness, closedness, perfectness, invariance, or density properties.
- Identifying the Julia set of a polynomial with the boundary of its filled Julia set.
- Computing or characterizing the Julia set of one concrete polynomial or rational map.

No family in this list is selected, asserted, or credited at intake.

## Explicit exclusions

The intake must not silently substitute complex dynamical systems (`THM-M-1427`), the Fatou set
(`THM-M-1429`), or the Mandelbrot set (`THM-M-1430`). The Douady-Hubbard theorem
(`THM-M-1431`), Yoccoz theorem (`THM-M-1432`), Sullivan no-wandering-domains theorem
(`THM-M-1434`), and McMullen theorem (`THM-M-1435`) also have separate repository roots.

Generic facts about analytic functions, iteration, periodic points, closures, frontiers, or complex
numbers do not identify this target. Nor do the Julia set of `z |-> z^2` chosen for convenience, a
definition repackaged as a theorem, a finite-orbit computation, an escape-time rendering, a
numerical fractal, or a structure that assumes the desired identity or density result as a field.
None can close an unspecified catalog object.

## Formal boundary

No canonical Lean expression is frozen. Pinned mathlib provides complex analytic functions,
function iteration, periodic points, topological closure and frontier APIs. A bounded source-name
search found no complex-dynamics Julia-set declaration. The only case-insensitive `Fatou` matches
found by the paired search concern Fatou's lemma in measure theory. These are substrate and search
facts only, not an exhaustive anchor audit, exact-statement elaboration, or machine-proof evidence.
