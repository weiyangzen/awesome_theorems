# Scope map

## Included subject

- A Riemannian-manifold volume-growth invariant defined from geodesic balls.
- A Laplace-Beltrami spectral quantity, fixed from a primary theorem rather than inferred from the
  repository title.
- A source-exact quantitative relationship, including every geometric and analytic hypothesis,
  normalization, constant, and limiting convention.

## Statement-phase decisions

The source must determine whether growth is polynomial, exponential, upper/lower, based at a point,
or uniform; whether the space is complete, compact, noncompact, a covering, or has boundary; and
whether the spectral object is `lambda_0`, a first positive eigenvalue, the essential-spectrum
bottom, or another endpoint. It must also fix the Laplacian sign, multiplicities, measure, ball
convention, limsup/liminf, zero/infinite growth, disconnected cases, and binder order.

## Exclusions and substitution guards

- Brooks's volume-growth/essential-spectrum theorem without evidence that this is not the separately
  listed `THM-M-1329`.
- A Cheeger, Cheng, Li-Yau, or Faber-Krahn inequality merely because it also mentions volume or an
  eigenvalue.
- A finite graph, matrix, or abstract compact-operator theorem substituted for the geometric claim.
- Any hypothesis or structure that assumes the desired spectral inequality.
- A convenient one-way corollary presented as an equivalence or as the unidentified root.
