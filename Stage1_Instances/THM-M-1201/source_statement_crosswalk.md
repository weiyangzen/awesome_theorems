The Stage0 phrase is therefore insufficient to freeze a theorem without inventing mathematics. The
statement phase is blocked until a primary source pinpoint resolves `SRC-ID` and `CLAIM-ID`. It must
then record the edition, theorem/page, all assumptions and errata, and test changed domain, removed
hypotheses, binder scope, constant states, smooth solutions, shocks, and initial-time boundaries.

The citations above are discovery anchors, not immutable evidence receipts. No external formal
artifact, Lean declaration, source-to-Lean equivalence, human-proof completeness, or machine closure
is claimed.
