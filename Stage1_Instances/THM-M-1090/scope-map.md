## Required statement decision

An accountable source review must identify one mathematical theorem, not merely the definition of
a Markov process. It must specify a process construction or hypotheses and a conclusion that the
resulting process satisfies one exact Markov predicate. The statement phase must then freeze all
ordered binders, exceptional-set conventions, and checked transports among any credited alternate
forms.

## Explicit exclusions

- Proving only that a supplied kernel is an `IsMarkovKernel`; that is a normalization property of a
  kernel, not the temporal conditional-independence claim.
- Assuming the desired Markov identity as a structure field and projecting it as the theorem.
- Substituting the adjacent SDE, Feller-semigroup, martingale-problem, Chapman-Kolmogorov, or
  Kolmogorov-equation targets.
- Replacing the general record by one finite-state example or by independent increments.
- Upgrading ordinary Markov to strong Markov without stopping-time and filtration hypotheses.
- Treating the historical attribution or `已验证` label as proof evidence.
