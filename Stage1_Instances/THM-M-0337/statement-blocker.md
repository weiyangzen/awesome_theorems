# Exact-statement blocker

The first failed rev-5.6 gate is source-statement identification. The repository supplies a name
for a cohomology theory and a broad subject gloss, but no quantified mathematical claim. An exact
Lean target cannot be created without choosing among materially different results and settings.

Retry after an authoritative source amendment or independently reviewed scope decision identifies
one proposition, the precise cyclic theory, algebra category, scalars and coefficients, all
hypotheses, the conclusion, and a pinpoint primary-source anchor. Until then, a Lean probe would
only test a worker-invented substitute and is therefore not valid statement evidence.
