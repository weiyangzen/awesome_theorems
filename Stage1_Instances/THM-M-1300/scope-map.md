# Scope map

## Included subject

- Triebel-Lizorkin spaces as a scale of function or distribution spaces.
- The dyadic decomposition, mixed sequence/function quasi-norm, admissible parameter ranges, and
  ambient domain needed by whichever exact source theorem is selected.
- A substantive structural result explicitly supported by that source, rather than the definition
  alone.

## Statement decisions still open

The source must fix homogeneous versus inhomogeneous spaces, `R^n` versus another domain, scalar
field, smoothness `s`, integrability parameters `p` and `q`, endpoint conventions, the resolution
of unity, and equality modulo polynomials where applicable. It must also identify what "refinement"
means: characterization, embedding, interpolation, lifting, atomic decomposition, or another
inequivalent theorem family.

## Explicit exclusions

- Treating existence of the definition as the target theorem.
- Substituting a theorem about Sobolev, Besov, or plain `L^p` spaces.
- Encoding the desired conclusion as a structure field or assumption.
- Treating the catalog's untrusted "verified" label as mathematical or kernel evidence.

Zero functions, `p` or `q` endpoints, homogeneous quotient conventions, and dependence on the
chosen dyadic partition must follow the exact selected source rather than be silently generalized.
