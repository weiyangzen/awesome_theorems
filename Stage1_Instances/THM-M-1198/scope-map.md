# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Subject: first-order hyperbolic partial differential equations.
- Technique: reduction along characteristic curves.
- Claimed outcome: a method for obtaining solutions.
- Historical scope: eighteenth-century work by multiple mathematicians; no named theorem or source.

This is all that the repository record fixes. It is not promoted to a theorem of existence,
uniqueness, representation, or regularity.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary theorem and freeze the equation (linear,
quasilinear, or fully nonlinear), domain and scalar field, coefficient regularity, initial or
boundary hypersurface and its noncharacteristic assumptions, data regularity, characteristic ODE,
existence interval, solution notion, uniqueness scope, representation formula, and all maximality
or blow-up conditions. Degenerate coefficients, zero transport field, characteristic initial data,
empty domains, and local-versus-global conclusions must be handled explicitly.

## Explicit exclusions

- Choosing the constant-coefficient transport equation merely because it has a convenient proof.
- Substituting an ODE flow theorem, transport identity, or uniqueness lemma for the unidentified PDE theorem.
- Treating the name of a technique or the metadata label `已验证` as a proposition or evidence.
- Claiming a general nonlinear result from a special linear characteristic formula.
