# Scope map

## Received claim

The repository supplies only:

- title: Cohen structure theorem;
- attribution: Irving Cohen;
- year: 1946;
- gloss: structure of complete Noetherian local rings;
- untrusted formalization status: verified.

This identifies a theorem family, not an exact truth-valued proposition. It does not define
completeness, select the maximal-ideal topology, state whether separatedness is included, or say
which structural output is intended.

## Candidate classical boundary

An inspected modern formulation, Stacks Project tag `032A` (Theorem 10.160.8), has two clauses:

1. every complete local ring has a coefficient ring;
2. if its maximal ideal is finitely generated, the ring is isomorphic to a quotient
   `Λ[[x_1, ..., x_n]] / I`, where `Λ` is a field or a Cohen ring.

The same section observes that a Noetherian complete local ring is therefore a quotient of a
regular local ring. This is a useful formulation lead, not the frozen target. The catalogue might
instead intend only the Noetherian specialization, only the power-series presentation, or a
historically different equal-characteristic or mixed-characteristic statement.

## Characteristic and output branches

Any exact statement must preserve rather than blur these branches:

- a coefficient field when the complete local ring contains a field (including equal
  characteristic zero or positive characteristic);
- a Cohen ring when the residue characteristic is `p > 0` and no power of `p` vanishes in the
  target ring;
- an Artinian coefficient ring with maximal ideal generated by `p` when the residue
  characteristic is positive and `p` is nonzero nilpotent in the target ring;
- existence of a coefficient ring versus existence of a surjective power-series map versus an
  isomorphism to its quotient;
- arbitrary complete local ring with finitely generated maximal ideal versus a Noetherian complete
  local ring, together with the proof that those hypotheses align;
- a quotient presentation versus the stronger regular-local or domain-normalization consequences.

## Pinned Lean boundary

At mathlib revision `8a178386ffc0f5fef0b77738bb5449d50efeea95`:

| Interface | What it supplies | Intake boundary |

| `MvPowerSeries σ Λ` | multivariate formal power-series ring | output-domain substrate only |
| `MixedCharZero R p` and `split_equalCharZero_mixedCharZero` | a characteristic-zero ambient-ring split into rational-algebra and mixed-characteristic cases | partial branch substrate only; it does not cover positive-characteristic ambient rings or the truncated `p`-nilpotent coefficient-ring case |

The bounded repository and pinned-mathlib search found no Lean declaration for coefficient-ring

The probe therefore supports M4, not M3 or proof credit.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

1. Preserve and hash a lawful primary or authoritative source edition and independently review the
   exact theorem passage, definitions, assumptions, conclusion, proof boundary, and errata.
2. Decide whether the target is the coefficient-ring clause, the power-series quotient clause,
   both as a conjunction, or the regular-local consequence.
3. Fix commutativity, identity, nontriviality, Noetherianity, local-ring bundling, and universes.
4. Fix completeness as an inverse-limit isomorphism or `IsAdicComplete` at the maximal ideal, and
   supply a checked transport if those encodings differ.
5. Define a coefficient ring in Lean, including completeness, maximal ideal, residue-field
   isomorphism, and the residue-characteristic condition.
6. Fix the coefficient-field, unramified Cohen-ring, and truncated `p`-nilpotent coefficient-ring
   branches; then fix variable index and finiteness, the ideal `I`, quotient and isomorphism
   category, and whether choices are existential data.
7. Resolve zero-variable, zero-dimensional, field, Artinian, characteristic zero/positive,
   imperfect residue-field, and degenerate ideal cases.
8. Elaborate one exact target, hash its expression and environment, check alternate transports, and
   mutation-test removed hypotheses, domain, binder scope, and boundary cases.

## Explicit exclusions

- The regular-local structure theorem, Cohen-Macaulay theorems, Cohen rings alone, or Cohen's
  theorem that every ideal of a commutative ring is finitely generated iff every prime ideal is.
- A power-series definition, adic-completion theorem, local-ring instance, or characteristic split
  used as if it were the structure theorem.
- A coefficient-field, Cohen-ring, or truncated `p`-nilpotent coefficient-ring branch used as the
  unrestricted theorem while dropping either of the other cases.
- A quotient presentation silently strengthened to an isomorphism with a power-series ring, which
  generally requires regularity.
- The regular-local consequence or a domain specialization substituted for the general root.
- Noetherianity, completeness, finite generation, separatedness, commutativity, or nontriviality
  inserted or removed without source authorization and checked transport.
- The catalogue `已验证` label, a theorem-name search hit, the `docs/1000.yaml` title, or a passing
  API probe used as statement identity or proof evidence.

No canonical Lean expression, ordered binders, complete hypothesis list, conclusion encoding,
alternate transport, or excluded degenerate case is frozen by this intake.
