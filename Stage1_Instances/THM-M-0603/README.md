# THM-M-0603 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake dossier for the catalog item "bordism theory." The received
claim is only "classification of manifolds by bordism," attributed to Rene Thom (1954). That phrase
does not determine one proposition: Thom's characteristic-number criteria differ between
unoriented and oriented smooth bordism, while the Pontryagin-Thom construction instead identifies
bordism groups with stable homotopy groups of Thom spaces.

The intake therefore freezes the literal claim and the admissible theorem family without choosing
one of those inequivalent results. The statement phase must first obtain a reviewed primary-source
pinpoint and decide category, orientation, dimension, characteristic-number coefficients, and
whether "classification" means a characteristic-number criterion or Pontryagin-Thom. Only then can
it freeze and elaborate an exact Lean proposition.

The provisional vector is `[H1, M4, R4]`. A primary publication and standard candidate theorem
families are identified, but the source-to-claim pinpoint, formal target, proof reconstruction, and
all completion gates remain open. No historical `已验证` label is proof credit. This intake claims
neither audit completion nor theorem completion.

The authoritative intake data are in `instance.json`; `scope-map.md` records inclusions and
exclusions, `source-statement-crosswalk.md` preserves source ambiguity, and `task-dag.json` keeps all
dependent work open. Exact validation commands and their limits appear in `validation.md` and the
worker receipt.
