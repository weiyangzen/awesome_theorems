# THM-M-1482 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the fail-closed `planned` intake dossier for the catalog label `遗传算法`
(genetic algorithm). The repository supplies only the gloss `基于进化的优化算法` (an optimization
algorithm based on evolution), attributes it to John Holland in 1975, and labels it `已验证`.
A method name and purpose do not form a truth-valued proposition with ordered binders,
hypotheses, and a conclusion. The verified label is untrusted metadata and supplies neither source
nor proof credit.

A genetic-algorithm theorem could mean Holland's schema result, preservation of population size,
well-definedness of a stochastic transition, convergence under elitism and mutation assumptions,
finite-state hitting probability, correctness of an implementation, or a runtime or approximation
bound. These alternatives require different genotype spaces, population representations, fitness
and selection rules, crossover and mutation kernels, replacement policies, randomness semantics,
stopping conditions, and conclusions. The catalog selects none of them. Choosing the familiar
schema theorem or a global-convergence result would invent proposition-changing mathematics.

Holland's 1975 book *Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems* is the natural historical
source lead matching the catalog author and year. His 1973 SIAM article *Genetic Algorithms and the
Optimal Allocation of Trials*, DOI `10.1137/0202009`, is another primary-source-family lead.
Neither work is cited by the catalog. No lawful immutable edition, exact theorem passage,
incorporated definition, assumption/proof boundary, correction record, or independent review has
been admitted, so neither receives `H0` credit.
