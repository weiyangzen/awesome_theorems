# Scope map

| Surface | Repository evidence | Intake boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Optimal transport; attributed to Gaspard Monge, 1781 | Historical attribution does not select a modern theorem |
| Human root | "the mathematical theory of the optimal transport problem" | Not proposition-shaped; exact root is open |
| Objects | masses, transport plans or maps, and a cost | Spaces, sigma-algebras, measures, and codomain are unspecified |
| Candidate claims | Monge/Kantorovich existence, Kantorovich duality, Brenier map structure, stability or regularity | No candidate receives statement or proof credit at intake |
| Lean surface | measure theory, probability measures, couplings, integrals, topology | No module or declaration is selected before source freeze |

Out of scope are toy finite assignment results, a one-dimensional rearrangement theorem, or a
generic minimum-on-a-finite-set lemma used as a replacement for the entry. Likewise, neighboring
manifest targets `THM-M-1184` (Kantorovich duality), `THM-M-1185` (Brenier theorem), and
`THM-M-1186` (optimal-transport existence) cannot be imported as the meaning of this target.

Statement freeze requires a theorem-level source pinpoint, exact spaces and measures, admissible
plans or maps, cost codomain and regularity, finiteness/moment conditions, and one explicit
conclusion. Degenerate feasibility and infinite-cost cases must be retained or excluded explicitly.
