# THM-M-1562 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake for the catalogue phrase "Tracy-Widom distribution:
random matrices and KPZ." The intended human claim is the common beta-2 Tracy-Widom limit in two
specific models: the soft-edge largest eigenvalue of normalized GUE matrices and the one-point,
long-time fluctuation of the narrow-wedge KPZ equation. The common law must be identified by a
concrete Airy-kernel Fredholm determinant, not by an opaque predicate that assumes either limit.

This scope is distinct from the earlier random-matrix-only target `THM-M-1107`. It does not claim
GOE/GSE laws, general random-matrix edge universality, or KPZ universality for arbitrary initial
data and models. Exact normalizations and primary-source theorem/equation/page anchors remain open
for the statement and source-audit phases.

The intake starts from `L0 / rework_required`, with provisional root vector `[H1, M4, R3]`. No
canonical Lean expression, accepted proof state, audit completion, or theorem completion is
claimed. The scope map, source crosswalk, and open task DAG record the downstream boundary.
