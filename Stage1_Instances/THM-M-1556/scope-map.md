# Scope map

## Supplied scope

The authoritative target manifest contributes only the label `孤立子理论` (soliton theory), the
category mathematical physics, and untrusted source status `已验证`. The Stage0 record adds the
gloss `孤立子的数学理论`, attribution to many mathematicians, and the twentieth century. These
fields identify a subject, not a truth-valued claim. In particular, they do not choose:

- an equation such as KdV, modified KdV, nonlinear Schrodinger, sine-Gordon, KP, or Toda;
- a meaning of soliton (explicit travelling wave, inverse-scattering datum, stable solitary wave,
  reflectionless potential, or asymptotic multi-soliton state);
- a result kind (existence, uniqueness, explicit solution, collision behavior, stability,
  completeness, or inverse-scattering reconstruction);
- real or complex domains, function spaces, regularity/decay, initial data, time direction, or
  coefficient and sign conventions.

Consequently there is no canonical statement, ordered binder list, hypothesis list, conclusion,
or boundary-case policy to freeze at intake.

## Required statement selection

The dependent statement phase must select exactly one proposition supported by a pinpoint primary
source. It must transcribe all assumptions and then freeze its PDE/ODE convention, solution notion,
function spaces, regularity and decay, parameter restrictions, time interval, and degenerate cases.
The selection must be reviewed as the intended meaning of this repository target; merely choosing
the easiest formally expressible fact is not sufficient.

Plausible but mutually non-equivalent source families include the KdV inverse-scattering
initial-value result, an explicit one-soliton KdV solution identity, an N-soliton construction, and
an orbital/asymptotic stability theorem. They are alternatives for target selection, not alternate
encodings of one frozen theorem, and none receives proof credit here.

## Explicit non-substitutions

- A proof that an assumed certificate implies its own packaged conclusion.
- The statement that the zero or a constant tau function solves a degenerate bilinear equation.
- A string-valued record of KdV conventions.
- A numerical recurrence observation presented as an analytic theorem.
- A generic travelling-wave identity presented as the whole of soliton theory.
- Any theorem belonging to neighboring targets for KdV, Hirota, Zakharov-Shabat, or AKNS.
