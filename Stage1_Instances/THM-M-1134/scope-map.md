# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Equation family: the heat equation, with its normalization and spatial domain unspecified.
- Object: a positive solution, with strict/nonnegative positivity and solution regularity unspecified.
- Result: a Harnack-type lower estimate, without regions, time slices, constant, or dependencies.
- Attribution: "many mathematicians", twentieth century; no primary theorem is named.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary source and freeze: spatial dimension and scalar field;
domain and time interval; heat-operator normalization; classical, weak, or distributional solution;
positivity convention; regularity and boundary assumptions; the earlier and later parabolic
cylinders or points; their compact containment and time separation; inequality direction; constant
and all dependencies; and local versus global scope. Degenerate dimension, empty regions, zero
solutions, boundary contact, and endpoint times must be addressed rather than silently excluded.

## Explicit exclusions

- Elliptic Harnack for harmonic functions, Nash-Moser divergence-form Harnack, or Krylov-Safonov.
- A mean-value formula, maximum principle, heat-kernel positivity, or Li-Yau estimate used as a
  substitute for the named result.
- An abstract proposition that assumes the desired Harnack inequality as a field.
- The metadata label `已验证` as source or kernel evidence.
