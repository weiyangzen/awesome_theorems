# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

The repository fixes only the label `重整化理论` ("renormalization theory"), the gloss
`动力系统的尺度变换` ("scale transformations of dynamical systems"), an attribution to many
mathematicians in the twentieth century, importance "high," and the untrusted status `已验证`.
It supplies no bibliography, theorem locator, definition, premise, conclusion, or formal artifact.
Intake therefore preserves only a dynamical-systems topic boundary involving changes of scale.

That boundary is not a proposition. In particular, it does not say what object is transformed,
what transformation is called renormalization, or what property of that transformation is asserted.

## Proposition-changing decisions

An approved source correction must select one immutable primary-source proposition and freeze:

- the dynamical category: interval or circle maps, real or complex analytic maps or germs,
  polynomial-like maps, rational maps, flows, or another exact class;
- the phase space, parameter space, universes, topology or analytic structure, regularity class,
  critical-point data, orientation, degree, and normalization conventions;
- the combinatorics and return data, including periodic, bounded, stationary, or arbitrary type,
  return times, restrictive domains, and existence of every required first-return map;
- the renormalization operation itself: iterate, restriction or induced map, affine or analytic
  coordinate changes, rescaling constants, sign and orientation conventions, and the domain on
  which the operation is total;
- the ambient operator space and metric, norm, manifold, or compactness structure when the claim
  treats renormalization as an operator;
- the exact conclusion: well-definedness, preservation of a class, fixed-point existence or
  uniqueness, convergence, compactness, hyperbolicity, a priori bounds, rigidity, universality, or
  another source-specified result;
- every ordered binder, local or global quantifier, constant and dependence clause, and implication
  direction; and
- all degenerate cases, including identity and constant maps, missing return domains, zero or one
  return time, zero scaling, orientation reversal, boundary critical points, and finite versus
  infinite renormalizability.

These choices yield inequivalent propositions. They are a statement-resolution checklist, not a
canonical claim.

## Candidate families not credited

- A source-specified definition and well-definedness theorem for an interval-map renormalization.
- Existence or uniqueness of a fixed point of a period-doubling renormalization operator.
- Hyperbolicity or spectral properties of such a fixed point.
- Real or complex a priori bounds and compactness of renormalizations.
- Rigidity or universality for infinitely renormalizable unimodal or quadratic-like maps.
- Renormalization for circle maps, rational maps, Hénon-like systems, flows, or statistical-
  mechanics models.

No family in this list is selected, asserted, or credited at intake.

## Explicit exclusions

The dossier must not replace this target with `THM-M-1437` Feigenbaum universality,
`THM-M-1438` Lanford's proof, or `THM-M-1439` Lyubich's proof. It must also remain distinct from
`THM-M-1427` complex dynamics, `THM-M-1430` the Mandelbrot set, `THM-M-1431` the
Douady-Hubbard theorem, `THM-M-1432` Yoccoz's theorem, and `THM-M-1435` McMullen's theorem.

Generic function-iterate or fixed-point lemmas, a conjugacy identity, a newly defined operator whose
desired property is a field, a finite numerical period-doubling experiment, and an approximate
scaling constant cannot identify or close the target. Nor can the catalog's `已验证` label, a book
title, or a paper about one candidate variant.

## Formal boundary

No canonical Lean expression is frozen. Pinned mathlib provides generic iteration,
semiconjugacy, fixed and periodic points, continuous maps, and homeomorphisms. A bounded source-name
search found no dynamical renormalization, Feigenbaum, unimodal-map, or quadratic-like-map target in
repo-local or pinned-mathlib Lean sources; the few `renormalization` text matches concern unrelated
peak functions. These are intake discovery facts only, not an exhaustive anchor audit,
exact-statement elaboration, or machine-proof evidence.
