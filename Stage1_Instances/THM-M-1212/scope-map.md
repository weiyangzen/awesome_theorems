# Scope map

## Included theorem family

- A nonlinear Schrodinger Cauchy problem, with equation normalization, spatial domain, dimension,
  sign, and nonlinearity fixed from one selected primary theorem.
- Initial data in the exact Sobolev, Lebesgue, or related function space used by that theorem.
- Existence on a nontrivial time interval, uniqueness in the stated solution class, and continuous
  dependence when all three are part of the selected well-posedness result.
- Maximal lifespan, blow-up alternative, conservation, global existence, or scattering only when
  explicitly included by the same selected theorem and with all additional hypotheses retained.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must choose a stable primary source and record its exact theorem and pages. It
must fix the equation, dimension, power or general nonlinearity, exponent range, focusing or
defocusing sign, initial-data and solution spaces, time interval, mild or strong solution notion,
all Strichartz or auxiliary norms, endpoint exclusions, and the precise topology for continuous
dependence. It must also map zero data, maximal-time endpoints, critical/subcritical regimes,
conservation assumptions, binder order, scalar field, and universes.

## Explicit exclusions

- The differential equation alone, without a quantified mathematical conclusion.
- A generic fixed-point theorem, linear propagator estimate, or an interface that assumes a
  solution as a field in place of NLS well-posedness.
- Substitution of the neighboring Ginibre-Velo (`THM-M-1213`) or Cazenave-Weissler
  (`THM-M-1214`) named result without a source-based identity argument.
- Global existence, scattering, unconditional uniqueness, or critical regularity inferred from a
  merely local or conditional source theorem.
- A theorem for a different dimension, exponent, domain, sign, or data space.

Because the repository phrase denotes a theory rather than a unique theorem, exact statement work
must resolve the ambiguity openly; intake does not silently broaden or narrow it.
