# THM-M-0715 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the fail-closed `planned` intake dossier for the repository label
"computability theory". The only local mathematical gloss is "the theory of computable
functions". That names a field, not a proposition with ordered binders, hypotheses, and a
conclusion.

The broader repository survey confirms the mismatch: its computability section lists many
different results, including undecidability, universality, Rice's theorem, recursion theorems,
reductions, and equivalences between machine models. Several of those also have their own manifest
records. Selecting any one of them as this root would broaden or substitute the assigned target.

The intake therefore freezes the ambiguity and scope boundary rather than inventing a theorem. The
root remains `[H3, M4, R4]`. A narrow pinned Lean probe confirms that mathlib exposes definitions of
primitive-recursive, partial-recursive, computable, computably enumerable, and Turing-machine
computability, together with representative theorems. These are candidate vocabulary and discovery
anchors only; they do not determine or prove a canonical claim. Exact commands and results are in
`validation.md`.
