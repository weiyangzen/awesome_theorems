# Scope map

## Included theorem family

- A first-order language `L`, an `L`-theory `T`, and a model satisfying `T` (or satisfiability of
  `T`, depending on the selected source theorem).
- Existence of a model realizing all complete types covered by an explicitly selected saturation
  convention.
- All language-size, target-cardinal, regularity, exponentiation, and model-cardinality conditions
  stated by the selected source theorem.

## Frozen scope fork

The Chinese source phrase means only "existence of saturated models" and does not distinguish:

1. existence of a `κ`-saturated elementary extension of a given model, without exact size;
2. existence of a saturated model of a complete/satisfiable theory at a prescribed size;
3. realization of unary/finite-tuple types versus all `<κ`-indexed tuple types;
4. saturation over subsets of the carrier versus named parameter maps (including repetitions).

The statement phase must select an inspected theorem and preserve its exact variant. Until then,
universes, binder order, completeness of `T`, regularity of `κ`, `|L| ≤ κ`, `κ ^< κ = κ`, exact
cardinality versus an upper bound, and treatment of finite cardinals remain open.

## Explicit exclusions

- Replacing saturation by mere compactness, consistency, model existence, or realization of one
  complete type in some model.
- Treating the desired saturated model as a field of an assumed package.
- Claiming the stronger legacy `<κ`-tuple/exact-cardinality candidate solely from the title.
- Counting homogeneity, universality, special models, or ultrapower saturation as the root theorem
  without a checked equivalence under the selected hypotheses.
