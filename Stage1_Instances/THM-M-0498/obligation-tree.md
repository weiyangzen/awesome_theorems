# THM-M-0498 frozen obligation architecture

Item: `S56-M-0498-OBLIGATION_TREE`.

The registry freezes 15 semantic obligations before proof execution. It uses
the classical Dirichlet-series, Perron inversion, contour-shift, and residue
route. The ordinary `pi` formula is explicitly excluded from the denominator:
it is a later transport, not a substitute for the weighted `psi` root.

## Typed proof route

```text
M0498-ROOT exact canonical proposition
`-- M0498-T-ASSEMBLE checked conditional composition
    `-- M0498-T-ANALYTIC pointwise analytic explicit-formula package
        |-- M0498-A-PERRON inverse-Mellin/Perron representation
        |   `-- M0498-A-DIRICHLET logarithmic-derivative identity
        |-- M0498-C-CONTOUR admissible contours and vanishing errors
        |-- M0498-B-RESIDUES pole, nontrivial, trivial, and zero residues
        |-- M0498-L-TRIVIAL trivial-zero series evaluation
        `-- M0498-L-ZERO-SUM ordered multiplicity-weighted convergence
```

Zero enumeration, endpoint conventions, foundation policy, primary sources,
provenance, and the excluded ordinary-prime-counting transport occupy separate
refinement, trust, provenance, documentation, and workflow graphs. They do not
silently become proof premises.

## Freeze boundary

The minimal open root cut is `M0498-T-ANALYTIC`. The checked conditional
assembly is not a proof of that premise. Each semantic node has a local budget
of at most 100 steps; exceeding it requires a versioned split. This phase
supplies no root closure, audit completion, or theorem completion. Registry
changes require a new version and append-only delta.
