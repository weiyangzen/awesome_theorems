## Modern statement-family lead

M. Yasir Kizmaz, *A generalization of Alperin fusion theorem and its applications*,
arXiv `2204.11303v2` (later DOI `10.1016/j.jalgebra.2024.02.016`), describes the
Alperin-Goldschmidt theorem as the special case in which every isomorphism between subgroups of a
finite `p`-group factors through restrictions of automorphisms of the whole group and essential
subgroups. Grazian and Parker, arXiv `2011.05011v2`, Theorem 5.9, similarly state that a saturated
fusion system is generated by automorphisms of its Sylow carrier and essential subgroups. These are
modern secondary/formulation witnesses. They neither resolve the catalog date/source identity nor
replace an accepted primary passage, so no canonical clause is selected from them.
