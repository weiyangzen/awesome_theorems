# THM-M-0064 scope map

## Catalog claim held fixed

The repository fixes the title `阿贝尔-鲁菲尼定理`, the attribution Niels Abel/Paolo Ruffini, the
year 1824, and the gloss `五次及以上一般多项式方程无根式解`. Intake preserves those strings and
does not turn the untrusted `已验证` label into source or machine evidence.

## Candidate theorem family

The conventional modern family connects solution by radicals to solvability of a polynomial's
Galois group and then shows that the relevant general degree-five or higher group is nonsolvable.
The catalog, however, does not choose between these materially different root shapes:

- no formula built from field operations and radicals uniformly expresses the roots of the
  generic degree-`n` polynomial for every `n >= 5`;
- the generic polynomial over a rational-function coefficient field has nonsolvable Galois group;
- for every natural `n >= 5`, there exists a degree-`n` polynomial over a fixed field, such as the
  rationals, whose roots are not all expressible by radicals; or
- one explicit quintic is not solvable by radicals, with higher degrees handled separately.

These candidates are discovery scope, not an accepted canonical statement.

## Decisions required at statement

- Admit and independently review an immutable primary statement, incorporated definitions, proof
  boundary, translation, and correction/errata record.
- Fix whether `general` means a generic polynomial, absence of a uniform formula, or existence of
  counterexamples; fix whether the root concerns degree exactly five or every degree at least five.
- Fix the coefficient/base field and ambient extension: rationals, characteristic zero, a field
  containing roots of unity, a rational-function field, an algebraic closure, or another source
  choice.
- Define solution of an equation versus solvability of a polynomial, one root versus all roots,
  and radical expressions versus a tower of radical extensions.
- Fix monic, irreducible, separable, and nonzero hypotheses and decide whether reducible
  polynomials are in the root scope.
- Fix the Galois-group formulation, the direction(s) of its equivalence with solvability by
  radicals, and the transport between a generic group and a symmetric group.
- Freeze ordered binders, hypotheses, conclusion, universes, typeclasses, and boundary cases; then
  elaborate and fingerprint one exact expression and run the required statement mutations.

## Boundary cases to resolve

- degrees `0` through `4`, and whether they appear only as an excluded boundary or a positive
  comparison theorem;
- degree exactly `5` versus each finite degree `n >= 5`;
- zero, constant, repeated-root, reducible, inseparable, already split, and solvable-Galois-group
  polynomials;
- characteristic zero versus positive characteristic, especially when the characteristic divides
  a radical exponent or the degree;
- a single radical-solvable root versus a full splitting field generated by radicals;
- adjoining roots of unity and whether intermediate extensions must be Galois; and
- generic coefficient specialization and exceptional coefficient loci.

## Excluded substitutions

- `isSolvable_gal_of_irreducible` presented as the full impossibility theorem: it is only a
  necessary-condition direction for a solvable-by-radicals root;
- `Equiv.Perm.fin_5_not_solvable` presented as a polynomial theorem without a checked Galois-group
  realization and source-faithful transport;
- one explicit quintic, irreducible polynomial, or special family silently replacing the catalog's
  word `general`;
- a degree-five-only theorem silently replacing all degrees at least five, or conversely;
- insolvability by rational functions, elementary functions, numerical algorithms, or special
  functions replacing insolvability by radicals;
- the unsolvability of one Galois group without a theorem connecting it to the selected polynomial;
- a hypothesis or structure that stores the desired nonsolvability conclusion; and
- the catalog label, theorem name, module title, or API probe used as proof credit.

## Neighbor boundaries

- `THM-M-0063` (Cayley's theorem) supplies a general permutation representation, not the
  nonsolvability or polynomial bridge needed here.
- `THM-M-0065` (Jordan-Hoelder) concerns composition-series invariance, not solution by radicals.
- `THM-M-0069` (Burnside theorem) is a group-solvability result under order restrictions, not the
  generic polynomial theorem.
- General Galois correspondence and finite-group solvability are dependencies or encodings only;
  neither automatically fixes this target's source scope.
