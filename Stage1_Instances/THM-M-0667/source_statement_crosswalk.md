# Source-statement crosswalk

The repository metadata names `阿克曼函数不可定义性` and summarizes it as
`原始递归函数的限制` (a limitation of primitive-recursive functions). That
wording does not specify a normalization or a formal meaning of definability.
The intake therefore freezes the standard two-variable Ackermann-Peter
non-primitive-recursiveness theorem and records the historical transport as
open rather than silently identifying different functions.

The 1928 title and the 1935 title use historical terminology whose literal
translation can be misleading under modern distinctions between recursive and
primitive-recursive functions. The source-audit phase must inspect the actual
editions, identify the exact result and notation, map the recursion equations
and function-class closure rules, check errata, and obtain independent review.
Until then the human-source status is `H1`, not `H0`.

The following are not interchangeable with the root without checked bridges:

1. Total/general recursiveness of `A` alone.
2. Non-primitive-recursiveness of only the diagonal `n |-> A(n,n)`.
3. Eventual domination by a fast-growing hierarchy without a proof that it
   covers every primitive-recursive function.
4. First-order undefinability in arithmetic or another model-theoretic
   structure.
5. Ackermann's original multi-variable function without a normalization
   transport.

No public Lean declaration or proof body is credited by this intake. The
statement and anchor-audit phases must search the repo-local tree, pinned
mathlib, and immutable external projects in the required order.
