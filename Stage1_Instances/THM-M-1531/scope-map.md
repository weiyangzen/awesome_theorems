# Scope map

## Included human claim

- A concrete gauge group and scalar representation act on a gauge-Higgs field model.
- The action or energy is gauge invariant and the scalar potential has a vacuum not fixed by the
  full gauge group.
- The vacuum stabilizer describes the unbroken subgroup.
- Expanding the covariant scalar kinetic term about the vacuum defines a gauge-field mass form or
  operator, with a nonzero conclusion for the broken directions under source-specific hypotheses.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must select one primary-source model and fix spacetime/sign conventions,
regularity, finite- versus infinite-dimensional field spaces, compactness and representation
hypotheses, the potential and vacuum conditions, gauge equivalence, and whether the conclusion is
positive mass on every broken direction, existence of a massive mode, or a rank/kernel identity.
It must also cover zero coupling, trivial action, empty broken sector, degenerate Hessian, and
residual symmetry. Universes, binder order, foundation profile, and imports follow that choice.

## Explicit exclusions

- The experimental discovery or measured mass of the Higgs boson.
- A global assertion about the full Standard Model without a frozen Lagrangian and regime.
- Goldstone's theorem, spontaneous symmetry breaking alone, or a toy scalar minimum as a substitute.
- A structure that assumes symmetry breaking, mass generation, or the target spectral fact as data.
- The legacy `StatementShape`, whose conclusion is projected from `HiggsMechanismData` fields.

The intended target is an axiomatized but substantive model theorem: assumptions may define the
model and analytic regime, but may not contain the desired stabilizer/mass conclusion.
