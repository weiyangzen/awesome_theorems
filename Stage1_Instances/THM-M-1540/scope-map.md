# Scope map

## Included claim

- The finite-dimensional classical twistor model over `C`, not twistor theory in full generality.
- Complexified compactified Minkowski spacetime and projective twistor space.
- The incidence relation customarily written `omega^A = i x^(AA') pi_A'` (up to the selected
  source's sign and index conventions).
- The point/line correspondence: a spacetime point determines a projective twistor line, and the
  converse holds under the precise admissibility conditions in the selected source theorem.

## Decisions required before statement elaboration

The statement phase must select a primary theorem and freeze homogeneous-coordinate quotients,
spinor dimensions and index conventions, the compactification model, the meaning of admissible
projective line, the real structure recovering real Minkowski space, and all degenerate cases
(`pi = 0`, zero homogeneous coordinates, points at conformal infinity). It must also decide whether
the source proves a bijection, an embedding, or two constructions inverse up to projective scaling.

## Explicit exclusions

- The slogan that all spacetime or all general relativity is "described by twistors".
- The nonlinear graviton theorem, Penrose transform, Ward correspondence, scattering amplitudes,
  or curved-spacetime generalizations as substitutes.
- Physical predictions, experimental observations, or an abstract structure that assumes the
  desired correspondence as a field.
- A toy linear-algebra incidence lemma presented as closure of the full source claim.

Until the source wording resolves the decisions above, the formalization debt is statement-level
and no theorem-tree denominator or proof credit is accepted.
