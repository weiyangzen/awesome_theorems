# THM-M-1543 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake for the Atiyah-Ward correspondence. It freezes the theorem
family as a correspondence between gauge-equivalence classes of anti-self-dual connections on a
four-dimensional conformal space and isomorphism classes of holomorphic vector bundles on its
twistor space satisfying line-triviality and a reality condition. The precise base, gauge group,
framing, regularity, charge, and source theorem remain statement-phase decisions.

only. Its abstract structures assume the missing transform data and do not establish the classical
correspondence. The Stage0 `已验证` label likewise supplies no proof credit. The provisional root
vector is `[H2, M4, R4]`; no canonical Lean target, accepted proof state, audit completion, or
theorem completion is claimed.

The scope map, source-statement crosswalk, and open task DAG define the downstream work. Exact
intake checks and their limits are recorded in `validation.md`.
