# Scope map

## Provisional root

- Objects: generalized Cartan matrices, with explicit diagonal, off-diagonal, zero-symmetry, symmetrizability, and indecomposability conditions.
- Construction: the Lie algebra defined by generators and Serre relations over a coefficient domain still to be selected.
- Comparison: matrix equivalence by reindexing versus an appropriately structured Lie algebra equivalence.
- Desired conclusion: freeze the exact direction(s), hypotheses, and equivalence notion justified by a primary source.

## Statement decisions still open

- Identify an exact published theorem rather than treating the named subject as a theorem.
- Determine field/ring, characteristic restrictions, index-set finiteness, and universe levels.
- Decide whether Cartan subalgebra, distinguished generators, grading, or triangular decomposition must be preserved. Abstract Lie algebra isomorphism may be too weak.
- Determine whether finite, affine, and indefinite types require separate claims and boundary cases.
- Freeze ordered Lean binders, imports, canonical expression, environment fingerprint, and checked transports.

## Explicit exclusions

- A classification of all infinite-dimensional Lie algebras.
- Classification of representations, integrable highest-weight modules, or characters.
- The finite-dimensional semisimple Lie algebra classification as a substitute.
- Construction alone (`Matrix.ToLieAlgebra`) as proof of a classification claim.
- The neighboring Kac-Peterson character formula target.
- Any legacy Lean declaration as rev-5.6 proof credit.

These exclusions prevent a named mathematical theory, a construction, or a nearby finite-type result from being silently substituted for an exact theorem.
