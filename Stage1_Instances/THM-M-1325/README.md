# THM-M-1325 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake for the Bishop-Gromov volume comparison theorem. The
human claim is the monotonicity of the ratio of geodesic-ball volume to the corresponding ball
volume in the simply connected constant-curvature model, under a Ricci lower bound.

The Stage0 phrase "Ricci curvature and volume" does not fix normalization, dimension, radii, or
cut-locus conventions. Those details are kept open for primary-source inspection rather than
silently selecting a nearby Bishop upper bound or a global volume estimate.

This intake claims only a dossier, scope map, source-statement crosswalk, and open downstream task
DAG. Its provisional root vector is `[H2, M4, R4]`; it claims no elaborated Lean declaration,
machine proof, audit completion, or theorem completion.
