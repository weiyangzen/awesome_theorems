# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Subject: a nonlinear Schrodinger equation (NLS) at an unspecified critical scaling.
- Claim: global well-posedness, with the precise existence, uniqueness, and dependence assertions
  unspecified.
- Attribution/time: Terence Tao, 2006, according to repository secondary metadata.

This is the entire defensible scope. "Critical" is not silently resolved to mass-critical or
energy-critical, and scattering is not silently added to global well-posedness.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary theorem and freeze the equation normalization, focusing
or defocusing sign, exponent, spatial dimension, real/complex scalar conventions, critical Sobolev
index, initial-data space, radial or symmetry assumptions, solution/mild-solution definition, time
interval, uniqueness class, continuous-dependence topology, conserved quantities, and whether the
conclusion includes spacetime bounds or scattering. It must map quantifiers and endpoint/zero-data
cases exactly and record edition, theorem/page, and errata.

## Explicit exclusions

- Choosing the energy-critical or mass-critical NLS solely from the year or author.
- Replacing global well-posedness by local well-posedness, small-data theory, conditional extension,
  or existence without uniqueness and continuous dependence.
- Adding scattering, radiality, or a dimension range unless the identified source theorem says so.
- Treating the metadata label `已验证` as source or kernel evidence.
- Reusing the Cazenave-Weissler candidate in `S1_M_153.lean` as Tao's theorem without a checked
  source identity; it concerns a separately attributed critical-NLS statement shape.
