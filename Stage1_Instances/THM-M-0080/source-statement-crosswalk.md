## Inspected primary source

Alexander Kurosch, *Die Untergruppen der freien Produkte von beliebigen Gruppen*, *Mathematische
Annalen* 109(1), 647-660 (1934), DOI `10.1007/BF01449159`, was inspected through the Göttingen
Digitization Centre's public IIIF scan and OCR on 2026-07-13. The volume manifest, complete article
PDF, printed-page-651 scan, page OCR, and Crossref record were hashed. The PDF is cited rather than
vendored because the archive's access terms restrict republication.

Section 2, `Untergruppensatz`, printed page 651 states:

> Jede Untergruppe F von G kann selbst in ein freies Produkt zerlegt werden, F = product over beta
> of F_beta, wo jeder Faktor F_beta entweder eine unendliche zyklische Gruppe oder mit einer
> Untergruppe von einer Komponente H_alpha konjugiert ist.

Faithful working translation: every subgroup `F` of `G` can itself be decomposed as a free product
`F = * F_beta`, where every factor `F_beta` is either an infinite cyclic group or conjugate to a
subgroup of one component `H_alpha`. The immediately preceding context assumes that `G` is the free
product of its component subgroups `H_alpha`. Footnote 5 says that the product may consist of only
one factor.

Section 1 on printed pages 649-650 introduces an arbitrary component family and the reduced-word
free product. The provisional proof-node map is:

- Section 2, pages 651-652: transfinite construction of subgroups `Phi_mu`, `K_nu`, and a factor
  given by the intersection of `F` with a conjugate of one component.
- Section 3, pages 653-655: Lemmas 1-2 and prime-word analysis establish the normal-form facts
  needed to show that each `Phi_nu` is the free product of the conjugate-intersection factor and
  infinite cyclic subgroups generated by chosen elements.
- Pages 655-657: induction hypothesis `(A)` for `K_nu` and Lemmas 3-6 control reduced halves,
  prime words, and transformations.
- Section 5, pages 658-660: Lemma 7 identifies the full conjugate intersection; Lemmas 8-10 and the
  successor/limit ordinal steps propagate `(A)` to `F`; page 660 explicitly says this proves the
  subgroup theorem and derives a freely-indecomposable corollary.
