# Scope map

## Metadata boundary

The repository supplies the name "strongly minimal theories", the phrase "classification of
strongly minimal theories", attribution to Boris Zilber, and the year 1984. It does not supply a
paper, theorem number, exact wording, language signature, cardinality convention, or definition of
"classification". The `已验证` label is untrusted metadata under rev-5.6 and supplies no source or
kernel credit.

## Candidate mathematical domains

A source-faithful target may need to quantify over a first-order language and a complete theory, or
over a definable set in a structure. It must freeze the convention that strong minimality means
every definable subset (with parameters, in every elementary extension or monster model according
to the selected source) is finite or cofinite. Depending on the selected theorem it may also need:

- algebraic closure as a pregeometry and its exchange property;
- dimensions, generic types, nonorthogonality, and coordinatization;
- local modularity, groups or fields interpretable in the structure, and precise bi-interpretability
  or nonorthogonality conclusions;
- countability of the language, model cardinalities, categoricity hypotheses, and spectrum claims;
- finite Morley rank/degree and saturation assumptions; and
- exceptional or degenerate cases such as finite models, named parameters, trivial geometry, and
  strongly minimal groups or fields.

## Variant decision required

At least four inequivalent roots fit the current gloss:

1. basic geometry of a strongly minimal set, such as algebraic closure forming a pregeometry;
2. a Baldwin-Lachlan categoricity/classification theorem relating uncountable categoricity to a
   strongly minimal core and dimensions of models;
3. Zilber's trichotomy proposal separating disintegrated, locally modular, and field-like geometry;
4. a valid trichotomy or classification theorem under extra hypotheses, such as a particular
   geometric, group, or analytic setting.

These have different truth status and formal types. Intake does not select among them.

## Explicit exclusions

- The unrestricted Zilber trichotomy as a proved theorem without addressing its counterexamples.
- A true special case with additional hypotheses not present in a selected source statement.
- The definition of strong minimality alone, or a tautological three-way predicate, as the
  classification theorem.
- A structure that stores the desired classification as a field.
- `THM-M-0679` or another separately scheduled target merged into this item.
- A statement about minimal theories, o-minimality, or categorical theories in general without a
  checked equivalence to the selected strongly minimal result.

The statement phase must freeze the exact source result, all definitions incorporated by reference,
ordered quantifiers, universe/cardinality conventions, boundary cases, foundation/choice profile,
and the precise formal meaning of every classification alternative.
