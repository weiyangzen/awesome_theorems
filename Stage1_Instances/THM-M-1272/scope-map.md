# Scope map

## Included claim

- An infinite-dimensional real Banach or Hilbert space with a countable decomposition into
  finite-dimensional directions and complementary tails.
- An even continuously Frechet-differentiable real functional.
- Fountain geometry: lower barriers on tail spheres tend to positive infinity, while suitable
  finite-dimensional sphere/ball boundaries have nonpositive upper bounds.
- A Palais-Smale condition at the minimax levels, or the exact compactness hypothesis of the
  selected classical source.
- Existence of critical values unbounded above, equivalently an infinite critical-point sequence
  whose functional values tend to positive infinity when the selected source states that form.

## Boundary decisions for the statement phase

Primary-source inspection must fix Banach versus Hilbert assumptions; the direct-sum/Schauder-basis
model; indexing and closure of tail spaces; the order and positivity of radii; strict versus weak
geometry inequalities; global Palais-Smale versus levelwise Palais-Smale; and whether the conclusion
is a sequence tending to infinity or merely unbounded critical values. It must also settle the zero
space, finite-dimensional, empty-sphere, and repeated-critical-point cases.

## Explicit exclusions

- The dual fountain theorem, generalized fountain theorem, or a Cerami variant as a substitute.
- A PDE corollary or a finite-dimensional symmetric criticality result in place of the abstract
  variational theorem.
- A structure that assumes the compactness/minimax conclusion as data.
- The legacy module's statement-shape wrappers as terminal proof evidence.

The later formal target must make the decomposition and geometric inequalities concrete rather than
hiding them in unconstrained `Prop` fields.
