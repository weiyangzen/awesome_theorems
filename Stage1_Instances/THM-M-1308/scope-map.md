# Scope map

## Included claim

- The `3+1` dimensional vacuum Einstein equations `Ric(g) = 0`.
- A vacuum initial data set on a three-dimensional hypersurface, close to Minkowski initial data in
  the weighted differentiability/decay regime required by the selected primary-source statement.
- Its maximal globally hyperbolic development.
- Global existence in the relevant evolution sense, causal geodesic completeness, and quantitative
  asymptotic stability/decay conclusions actually asserted by the source.

## Decisions deferred to statement freeze

The exact source passage must determine the topology and asymptotic ends of the initial
hypersurface; vacuum constraint equations; differentiability order; weights and smallness norm;
normalization of ADM quantities; maximal-development interface; time orientation; future versus
past completeness; and the precise curvature, foliation, and asymptotic estimates included in the
terminal claim. Degenerate zero data and coordinate/gauge encodings require explicit treatment.

## Explicit exclusions

- Linear stability of the wave equation or linearized Einstein equations.
- Stability of Schwarzschild, Kerr, de Sitter, or higher-dimensional spacetimes.
- A local well-posedness theorem without global completeness and decay.
- A statement that assumes global existence, completeness, or the required estimates as fields of
  an abstract package.
- Replacing the book theorem by a later weaker small-data theorem without checked implication.

The eventual Lean statement must expose concrete Lorentzian geometry, vacuum constraints,
development, small weighted initial-data norms, and completeness/asymptotic predicates, or record
the exact missing API as a formalization blocker.
