# Scope map

## Included source claim

- A bulk theory with gravity/string degrees of freedom on an asymptotically anti-de Sitter space.
- A conformal quantum field theory on the conformal boundary.
- An equivalence/dictionary matching bulk fields and boundary operators and, in the GKP/Witten
  formulation, bulk partition functions with boundary generating functionals.
- Model, dimension, coupling/large-`N` regime, boundary conditions, and equality notion as explicit
  parameters or hypotheses once a primary formulation is selected.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must select one source formulation and distinguish an exact correspondence
claim from limits or checks. It must fix the bulk/background (for example the type-IIB
`AdS5 x S5` case), boundary CFT, classical/supergravity versus full quantum regime, observable
class, regularization and renormalization assumptions, and whether equality means partition-
function equality, correlation-function equality, or categorical/unitary equivalence. These
choices determine all Lean universes, binders, hypotheses, and the conclusion.

## Explicit exclusions

- Replacing the correspondence by a finite-dimensional linear equivalence or an abstract structure
  that contains the desired equivalence as a field.
- Proving only an analysis, Hilbert-space, spectral, variational, or special-parameter lemma and
  presenting it as the full correspondence.
- Treating physical evidence, a large-`N` approximation, or a protected-observable match as proof
  of the unrestricted conjecture.
- Substituting dS/CFT, flat-space holography, the holographic principle in general, or a tensor
  network analogy.
