# THM-M-0709 frozen obligation architecture

Item `S56-M-0709-OBLIGATION_TREE` freezes registry version 1 against the exact
`Statement.lean` and `anchor-audit.json` hashes in `obligation-registry.json`.
The 18 canonical IDs are the denominator for later machine, human-source, and
readable coverage. A correction, split, merge, eligibility change, or different
reduction architecture requires a versioned append-only delta.

## Proof architecture

The selected route reduces the pinned halting problem to the exact structured
binary PCP predicate. It separates machine normalization, modified-PCP tile
construction, both semantic directions, the modified-PCP to ordinary-PCP
transport, both ordinary-PCP directions, a self-synchronizing binary alphabet
encoding, computability of the complete reduction, and the final pullback of a
hypothetical decider. This prevents a citation to the classical reduction from
concealing its constructions, invariants, case directions, or encoding boundary.

The proof graph has reciprocal `proof_requires` and `composes` edges. Separate
refinement, provenance, evidence, trust, documentation, and workflow graphs keep
non-proof support from contributing machine closure. Every node has a semantic
ledger and a leaf split budget no greater than 100 steps.

## Boundary

`ObligationTree.lean` repeats the frozen definitions (because the dossier is
outside the Lake module tree) and checks an identity harness at the exact root
type. The statement validator separately guards exact source equivalence. It does
not implement any reduction child and earns no proof or composition credit. No
terminal proof body is registered. The root remains `M3`, the source pinpoint
review remains open, readability remains `R3`, and theorem completion is false.
The first open cut is the halting wrapper, machine normalization, modified-PCP
construction, source map, and foundation audit. Master acceptance is still required.
