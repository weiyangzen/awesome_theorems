# Scope map

## Included theorem boundary

- A source-pinned first-order signature and treatment of equality.
- A source-pinned formula class and transformation to prenex, Skolem, universal, or clause form.
- The Herbrand universe, ground terms, ground instances, and Herbrand interpretations generated by
  that signature, including any fresh constant convention.
- One exact validity, satisfiability, or refutability conclusion, with both directions when the
  selected source states an equivalence.
- All finiteness, nonemptiness, classical-logic, and proof-system hypotheses used by that result.

## Ambiguities to resolve at statement freeze

1. For an existential formula, validity is characterized by a finite disjunction of ground
   instances of its quantifier-free matrix.
2. A universal Skolem form is unsatisfiable exactly when some finite set of its ground instances is
   propositionally unsatisfiable.
3. A set of clauses is satisfiable exactly when it has a Herbrand model.
4. A syntactic Herbrand-Gentzen theorem formulated through a particular sequent calculus.

The statement phase must select one immutable source passage and freeze the ordered binders,
signature arities, formula representation, semantics, equality policy, normal-form transports,
Herbrand construction, hypotheses, and conclusion. Other formulations may receive credit only
through checked transports.

## Explicit exclusions

- `Classical.skolem` or mathlib's model-theoretic `Language.skolem₁` alone as a substitute for
  Herbrand's theorem.
- General first-order compactness, completeness, or Lowenheim-Skolem as automatic synonyms.
- A finite ground-instance search that assumes the desired completeness theorem.
- A propositional tautology theorem with the first-order grounding bridge omitted.
- The repository label `已验证` as human-source or kernel evidence.

No canonical Lean target is frozen at intake because the repository record does not identify one.
