# Scope map

## Preserved subject family

The intake preserves exactly the recursion-theory subject family `properties of creative sets`.
A later statement phase may select a root only after an immutable source passage is mapped and
independently reviewed. Candidate components, none credited as the theorem at intake, include:

- a set or predicate on Post's positive integers or on Lean natural numbers;
- computable enumerability of that set;
- Post's effective diagonalizing function from an index or basis for a c.e. subset of the
  complement to a fresh element of the complement;
- existence of a creative set, with Post's complete set `K` as a witness;
- noncomputability and complement-infinitude consequences;
- many-one or one-one completeness for c.e. predicates; and
- modern equivalences between creativity, productivity of the complement, and c.e.-completeness,
  each requiring a checked source and representation crosswalk.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

1. Select one exact source proposition, or obtain approval for an explicitly multi-root package;
   do not turn the catalog's plural family into an invented conjunction.
2. Fix positive integers versus `Nat`, including the role of zero and a checked transport.
3. Choose a predicate, `Set Nat`, range, partial-function domain, or index-set representation.
4. Select the computable-enumerability model and the effective numbering of c.e. predicates.
5. Freeze Post's `basis B` and recursive witness-function interface, including what the function
   must do on indices that do not enumerate a subset of the complement.
6. Fix the complement convention, subset relation, freshness conclusion, effectiveness and
   uniformity requirements, ordered binders, and all implicit decidability or coding instances.
7. If a completeness result is selected, fix the reduction notion and direction, the quantified
   source class, extensional equality convention, and relationship to the particular complete set
   `K` or a modern halting predicate.
8. Record the entire imported definition chain, proof boundary, corrections and errata, foundation
   policy, and independent source approval.

## Boundary and degenerate cases

Source review must explicitly dispose of the empty set, universal set, finite candidate sets,
empty or malformed c.e. indices, duplicate indices for extensionally equal predicates, a witness
function used outside its conditional specification, positive-integer/Nat boundary behavior, and
whether a chosen completeness statement ranges over all predicates or only c.e. predicates.

## Explicit exclusions

- `THM-M-0747` simple-set existence, `THM-M-0748` Post's problem, and
  `THM-M-0749` Friedberg-Muchnik incomparability are distinct roots.
- The computer-science catalog row `创造集与单纯集` states only existence of a nonrecursive c.e.
  set. That is weaker than creativity and belongs to a separate record.
- A generic noncomputable c.e. predicate, the halting problem, Post's theorem on c.e./co-c.e.
  predicates, Rice's theorem, or a many-one reduction API cannot substitute for a creative-set
  theorem without checked source-specific bridges.
- Mathlib's `WSeq.Productive` concerns non-stalling weak sequences, not productive complements in
  computability theory.
- A structure or hypothesis that stores the creative witness/function cannot prove existence by
  projecting the stored field.
- The catalog's untrusted `已验证` label and the intake probe supply no human-source or machine-proof
  credit.

No canonical Lean target, statement fingerprint, alternate encoding, obligation registry,
discovery protocol, proof state, or completion claim is frozen at intake.
