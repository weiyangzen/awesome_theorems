The repository research note attributes the theorem to Kiyosi Ito (1951) and summarizes it only as
"the stochastic-integral representation of Brownian martingales." That metadata is a discovery
lead, not a primary-source statement or an accepted historical attribution. The source audit must
identify an exact original or authoritative theorem, immutable edition, page/theorem number,
assumptions, corrections/errata, and a node-level premise crosswalk before `H0` is possible.

The existing Lean module explicitly says that its stochastic-integral API is a boundary. Moreover,
its `BrownianRepresentationHypotheses` includes `StochasticIntegralRepresentation`, the same
existential content as its conclusion. Thus its checked implication is circular as a model of the
root theorem and supplies no machine-proof credit. It may inform vocabulary only.
