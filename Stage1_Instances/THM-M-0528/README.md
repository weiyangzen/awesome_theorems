# THM-M-0528 rev-5.6 intake

This directory began as the `planned` intake for uniqueness of lifts through a covering map. The
repository title `单值化定理` can also denote the analytic uniformization theorem, but its own
gloss, `覆盖空间的提升唯一性`, selects the covering-space lifting subject instead.

The statement phase selects the general theorem: two continuous lifts from a preconnected domain
are equal when their projections are equal and they agree at one specified point. The exact Lean
target and its validation record are in `Statement.lean`, `statement.json`, and
`statement-validation.md`. Primary-source acceptance, proof credit, audit completion, and theorem
completion are not claimed.
