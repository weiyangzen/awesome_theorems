# THM-M-0528 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake for uniqueness of lifts through a covering map. The
repository title `单值化定理` can also denote the analytic uniformization theorem, but its own
gloss, `覆盖空间的提升唯一性`, selects the covering-space lifting subject instead.

The intake freezes only that subject. It does not yet choose between path-lift uniqueness and the
more general theorem that two continuous lifts from a connected domain agree everywhere when they
agree at one point. That choice, and the exact source edition and hypotheses, belongs to the
statement phase. The provisional root vector is `[H3, M4, R4]`; no exact statement, proof credit,
audit completion, or theorem completion is claimed.
