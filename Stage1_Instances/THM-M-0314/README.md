# THM-M-0314 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the fail-closed `planned` intake dossier for the repository claim "spectral
decomposition of compact self-adjoint operators". The claim identifies a standard theorem family,
but the supplied metadata does not fix a unique proposition: it gives no scalar field, Hilbert-space
hypotheses, decomposition encoding, treatment of the zero eigenspace, or source theorem/page.

The intake therefore freezes the literal claim and its ambiguity boundary without selecting one
formal variant. The root remains `[H3, M4, R4]`. A pinned Lean probe confirms that mathlib contains
two directly relevant compact self-adjoint results: completeness of the family of eigenspaces and
finite-dimensionality of every nonzero eigenspace. Those checks are candidate anchors only, not an
exact statement certificate or proof credit. Exact commands and results are in `validation.md`.
