# Scope map

## Frozen catalog scope

- Attribution: Serge Alinhac.
- Catalog date: 1986 (unverified against a primary publication).
- Subject: singularities of nonlinear hyperbolic equations.
- Intended result type: a theorem about singularity formation or blow-up, not merely local
  well-posedness or a definition of a hyperbolic operator.

This is the maximum truthful scope supported by the repository source record. It is not yet a
mathematical statement.

## Decisions required before statement elaboration

The statement phase must identify a primary publication and exact theorem/page, then freeze: the
scalar or system PDE; space dimension and domain; operator and hyperbolicity assumptions;
quasilinear/semilinear regime; initial-data regularity, size, support, and sign/nondegeneracy
conditions; lifespan quantifiers; the meaning and locus of singularity; and whether the conclusion
is finite-time blow-up, an upper lifespan bound, geometric blow-up, or propagation of an existing
singularity. It must also reconcile the catalog date with the publication history and errata.

## Explicit exclusions

- Choosing a familiar Alinhac blow-up theorem solely because its title resembles the catalog text.
- Replacing the target by a generic ODE blow-up lemma, local existence theorem, energy estimate, or
  an abstract structure that assumes singularity formation.
- Treating another author's nonlinear wave blow-up theorem as an equivalent result.
- Crediting the untrusted catalog status `已验证` as mathematical or kernel evidence.

Until the identification gate closes, binder order, universes, boundary cases, foundation profile,
and a Lean module/declaration remain deliberately open.
