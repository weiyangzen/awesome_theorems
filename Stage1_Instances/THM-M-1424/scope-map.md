# Scope map

## Catalog scope preserved

- Target identity: `THM-M-1424`, named `随机动力系统` (random dynamical systems).
- Catalog attribution and date: Ludwig Arnold, 1998.
- Literal gloss: `随机微分方程的动力学` (dynamics of stochastic differential equations).
- Subject boundary: random dynamical systems related to random or stochastic differential
  equations.

This is all the mathematical scope fixed by the repository. It does not determine a theorem.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

An approved target correction must select one immutable source proposition and freeze:

- the noise base: probability space, measurable metric dynamical system, base flow or shift,
  invariance, time direction, completion, and almost-everywhere convention;
- the time object: discrete or continuous, monoid or group, one-sided or two-sided, and the
  treatment of time zero and negative time;
- the state space and universes, including measurable, topological, metric, manifold, or Banach
  structure and any separability, completeness, compactness, or finite-dimensional assumptions;
- the random dynamical object: global or local cocycle, crude versus perfected cocycle, measurable,
  continuous, or smooth dependence, explosion/lifetime conventions, and exceptional null sets;
- for an equation-generated result, the driver and integration theory, coefficient regularity,
  solution concept, pathwise uniqueness, maximal/global existence, and modification/version policy;
- whether the equation is a pathwise random differential equation or a stochastic differential
  equation, whether the claim is forward generation, converse representation, or a paired
  correspondence, and whether the driver is Brownian or a general semimartingale helix;
- the exact conclusion: existence of a cocycle, cocycle identity/perfection, generation or converse
  representation, continuity/smoothness, invariant measure, stability, or another named result;
- every ordered binder, hypothesis, constant dependency, equality scope, and boundary case.

These choices yield inequivalent propositions. They are a resolution checklist, not a canonical
claim.

## Candidate source families not credited

- The definition and basic properties of a global random dynamical system or cocycle in Arnold
  1998, Section 1.1.
- The perfection of a crude cocycle generated by a stochastic differential equation, summarized
  as Theorem 1.3.2 and its corollaries.
- The global or local theorem that a random differential equation generates an absolutely
  continuous random dynamical system, and its converse.
- The global or local theorem that a semimartingale-driven stochastic differential equation
  generates a semimartingale random dynamical system, and its converse.
- Existence, invariance, ergodicity, stability, differentiability, or long-time behavior for a
  source-specified subclass of random dynamical systems.

No family in this list is selected, stated, or credited at intake.

## Boundary and degenerate cases

The statement phase must resolve trivial probability or state spaces, deterministic zero-noise
systems, zero time, empty or singleton state spaces, local solutions with finite explosion time,
nonunique solutions, null exceptional sets that depend on time or state, indistinguishable versus
pathwise-equal versions, and one-sided systems without inverse base shifts. It must also distinguish
joint measurability from separate measurability and a perfect cocycle identity from an identity that
holds only almost surely for each fixed parameter.

## Explicit exclusions

- Random attractors (`THM-M-1425`), multivalued random dynamical systems (`THM-M-1426`), and the
  synchronization/coupling target (`THM-M-1423`) are separate roots.
- A definition of a stochastic process, filtration, adaptedness, Markov kernel, measure-preserving
  map, or deterministic flow is substrate, not this missing theorem.
- A deterministic ODE flow, a finite stochastic simulation, a numerical trajectory, or a generic
  Markov-chain result cannot substitute for an SDE/RDS theorem.
- A structure that assumes the desired cocycle, solution, invariant measure, or conclusion as a
  field does not prove its construction or existence.
- The catalog label `已验证` supplies neither human-source nor Lean kernel evidence.

## Formal boundary

No canonical Lean expression is frozen. Pinned mathlib exposes filtrations, adapted stochastic
processes, Markov kernels, measure-preserving maps, and deterministic topological flows. The bounded
intake search found no named random-dynamical-system, stochastic-differential-equation,
semimartingale, or metric-dynamical-system interface. These are discovery facts only, not a complete
anchor audit, exact-statement elaboration, or proof.
