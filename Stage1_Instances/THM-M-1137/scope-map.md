# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Subject: harmonic functions.
- Claimed relation: a value of a harmonic function is related to an average of that function.
- Historical metadata: nineteenth century and attributed only to "many mathematicians".
- Logical character: the wording names a theorem family but supplies no quantifiers or formula.

Nothing in the repository source determines which standard mean-value formulation is intended.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary source and freeze the ambient dimension and normed
space, scalar codomain, open domain, harmonicity and regularity predicates, center, positive radius,
and the closure/containment condition for the ball. It must select an average over a sphere, over a
ball, or a stated equivalence, and specify surface/volume measure and normalization. It must also
handle dimension zero, radius zero, empty or disconnected domains, boundary contact, measurability,
and integrability explicitly rather than silently excluding them.

## Explicit exclusions

- The converse theorem that a continuous function with the mean-value property is harmonic.
- Subharmonic inequalities, weighted means, manifolds, discrete harmonic functions, heat kernels,
  and probabilistic stopping-time variants unless a primary source selects them.
- Replacing the normalized mean by an unnormalized integral or swapping ball and sphere averages.
- Treating the metadata label `已验证` or a neighboring harmonic-function target as evidence.
