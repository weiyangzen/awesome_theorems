# Scope map

## Included claim family

- A sequence associated with a specified critical variational PDE and bounded in its energy space.
- Passage to a subsequence, a weak or background limit, and finitely many nontrivial concentrated
  solutions obtained by recentering and rescaling.
- Pairwise asymptotic separation of concentration parameters.
- A remainder vanishing in the exact topology stated by the selected source.
- Energy or norm decoupling only when it is part of that same theorem.

## Decisions required before statement elaboration

The primary theorem must fix the equation and domain, dimension, boundary conditions, critical
exponent, coefficient and regularity assumptions, the boundedness or Palais-Smale hypothesis, the
notion of solution, all convergence topologies, whether boundary bubbles occur, finiteness, scale
and center conventions, and the precise remainder and energy identities. It must also settle whether
the result concerns Euclidean elliptic equations, manifolds, maps, or another critical system.

## Explicit exclusions

- A generic finite-dimensional sequence decomposition or an arbitrary structure containing bubbles.
- A linear profile decomposition substituted for a nonlinear bubble theorem.
- Concentration-compactness stated only as tightness/vanishing/dichotomy.
- Harmonic-map bubble trees, Yamabe/Sobolev extremals, or critical wave/Schrodinger profiles unless
  the inspected primary source identifies that exact theorem.
- The adjacent Stage0 item `THM-M-1296` (profile decomposition) as an interchangeable result.

Until the source is selected, binder order, universes, degenerate cases, classical-choice use, and
the concrete Lean analysis APIs remain deliberately open rather than invented.
