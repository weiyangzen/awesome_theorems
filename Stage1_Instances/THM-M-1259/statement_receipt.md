# S56-M-1259-STATEMENT receipt

## Frozen target

`Statement.lean` elaborates the proposition
`Stage1Instances.THM_M_1259.hormanderTarget`. It formalizes the 1967 sum-of-squares theorem in
Euclidean coordinates: for smooth real fields `X_0, X_1, ..., X_r` and a smooth zeroth-order
coefficient `c` on an arbitrary open subset of `Real^n`, full pointwise rank of the Lie algebra
generated by all these fields implies hypoellipticity of
`P = sum_j X_j^2 + X_0 + c`.

The target quantifies over a bundled formal transpose on test functions. This makes the
distributional equation exact without presuming unimplemented compact-support closure lemmas for
variable-coefficient differentiation. Smooth distributions are explicitly distributions induced
by smooth densities; `IsHypoelliptic` says smoothness of `P T` implies smoothness of `T`.

Domains and binders are ordered in `hormanderTarget`: dimensions; open domain; drift, square, and
zeroth-order coefficients; Lebesgue measure; formal-adjoint realization; coefficient smoothness;
bracket condition; conclusion. `n = 0`, `r = 0`, and the empty domain are intentionally not silently
excluded: the rank premise and implication determine those boundary cases.

Minimal direct imports are:

- `Mathlib.Analysis.Calculus.VectorField` for `VectorField.lieBracket`;
- `Mathlib.Analysis.Distribution.Distribution` for test functions and distributions;
- `Mathlib.MeasureTheory.Integral.Bochner.Basic` for the density integral and measure.

The source selection is Hormander, *Hypoelliptic second order differential equations*, Acta
Mathematica 119 (1967), 147-171, DOI `10.1007/BF02392081`, rather than a later boundary theorem or
an estimate substituted for the named regularity result. On 2026-07-12 the publisher and Project
Euclid PDF endpoints returned anti-automation HTML, so source-page transcription and errata review
remain H-status work for the later source audit. This does not prevent truthful freezing of the
standard theorem target, but it prevents an H0 claim.
