# THM-M-0549 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake for the cup product. The Stage0 wording, "the structure of
the cohomology ring", is treated as a theorem-family label rather than as an exact proposition.
The intended human claim is that ordinary singular cohomology with a commutative coefficient ring
has the natural graded-ring structure induced by cup product, including associativity, a unit, and
graded commutativity. The precise space and coefficient hypotheses and the exact primary-source
statement remain statement-phase decisions.

No canonical Lean expression has been selected or elaborated. In particular, an arbitrary binary
operation packaged with the desired laws would be circular and is explicitly excluded. The
provisional root vector is `[H1, M4, R4]`; no accepted proof state, audit completion, or theorem
completion is claimed.

The scope map, source-statement crosswalk, and open task DAG record the downstream work. Intake
checks and their exact results are recorded in `validation.md`.
