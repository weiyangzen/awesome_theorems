# Scope map

## Included claim

- A defocusing semilinear nonlinear wave equation on Euclidean space at energy-critical scaling.
- Initial position and velocity in the finite-energy data space.
- Global-in-time existence and uniqueness in an energy/critical spacetime solution class.
- Persistence of the energy regularity and the continuous-dependence component only if present in
  the selected primary statement.

## Decisions reserved for the statement phase

The primary text must settle whether the canonical theorem is the three-dimensional quintic
equation or a dimension-general critical-power formulation; whether data are arbitrary energy data
or initially smoother data followed by completion; the sign and normalization of the wave
operator; real- versus complex-valued fields; the exact weak/strong solution class; and whether
scattering, energy conservation, and continuous dependence are conclusions of this theorem or
separate results. Ordered binders, time interval conventions, and degenerate/zero-data cases must
follow that source audit.

## Explicit exclusions

- The focusing equation, where finite-energy blow-up can occur.
- Subcritical local well-posedness as a substitute for critical global well-posedness.
- Small-data global existence as a substitute for arbitrary finite-energy data.
- A classical-solution theorem for smooth compactly supported data without the energy-space
  extension.
- Scattering alone, a priori spacetime bounds alone, or an abstract structure that assumes the
  desired global solution.

The future Lean statement must expose the wave equation, initial trace, solution space, uniqueness
class, and global time domain concretely, or record a precise missing-analysis-API blocker.
