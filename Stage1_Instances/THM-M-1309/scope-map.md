# Scope map

| Surface | In scope | Intake boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Equations | Vacuum Einstein equation `Ric(g) = 0` | No cosmological constant or matter field |
| Data | Small asymptotically flat constraint-satisfying perturbations of Minkowski data | Source-exact weighted norms, decay orders, differentiability, and mass term await transcription |
| Gauge | Harmonic/wave coordinates used for the reduced evolution | A gauge condition by itself is not the root claim |
| Analytic result | Global small-data evolution and decay/asymptotics | Local well-posedness or reduced-system existence alone is insufficient |
| Geometric result | Recovery of the full Einstein equations and future causal geodesic completeness | Past completeness and arbitrary large data are excluded |
| Formal surface | One exact Lean proposition encompassing data, evolution, decay, and completeness | No candidate declaration or adequate prerequisite stack is presently known |

The statement phase must decide whether to encode maximal globally hyperbolic development or the
wave-coordinate development constructed in the paper, and must prove their relationship if both
appear. It must not silently replace causal geodesic completeness by coordinate-time existence.
