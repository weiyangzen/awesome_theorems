# THM-M-1252 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake for the support/localization of distributions. The Stage0
record supplies only the title "support of a distribution" and the phrase "localization of a
distribution"; it does not state a unique theorem. Accordingly, this intake freezes the subject
boundary, but deliberately leaves selection of the exact source proposition to the statement phase.

The intended claim family is the classical localization characterization: the complement of the
support of a distribution is the largest open set on which its restriction vanishes. Whether the
eventual root is that maximal-open-set statement, the equivalent test-function characterization,
or a restriction/gluing theorem must be decided from an inspected primary source. No convenient
variant is silently substituted here.

The provisional vector is `[H2, M4, R4]`. There is no canonical Lean expression, accepted proof
state, audit completion, or theorem completion.
