# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Named result: "Segal theorem".
- Attributed author and year: Irving Segal, 1963, from unverified secondary metadata.
- Subject: local well-posedness of a nonlinear wave equation (`NLW`).
- Repository category: differential equations / partial differential equations.

This is the entire justified mathematical scope. "Local well-posedness" suggests existence,
uniqueness, and stability in some functional setting, but the record does not state any of those
clauses precisely enough to freeze them.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify the primary paper and exact theorem/page, then freeze the spatial
domain and dimension, scalar field, wave operator and sign convention, nonlinearity and parameter
range, initial-data space, solution class, weak/mild/strong formulation, lifespan and its dependence
on data, uniqueness class, topology and strength of continuous dependence, and all endpoint,
zero-data, and maximal-interval cases. Ordered binders and quantifier scope must follow that source.

## Explicit exclusions

- Substituting a generic abstract semilinear evolution fixed-point theorem.
- Substituting the later Ginibre-Velo, Shatah-Struwe, Grillakis, or Tao NLW result.
- Choosing a power nonlinearity, dimension, Sobolev exponent, or energy regime from modern usage.
- Treating existence alone, uniqueness alone, or an a priori estimate as well-posedness.
- Treating the repository label `已验证` as human-proof or kernel evidence.
