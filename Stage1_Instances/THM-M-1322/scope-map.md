# Scope map

## Included subject

- A bounded domain in finite-dimensional Euclidean space.
- The Laplacian with Neumann boundary condition and its discrete eigenvalue sequence.
- An upper bound attributed to Pedro Kroger, with dimension, volume, index, and numerical constants
  copied exactly from the selected primary theorem.

## Decisions required before the statement phase can close

The primary text must decide the regularity and connectedness of the domain, the meaning of
"domain", whether eigenvalues are counted with multiplicity, whether indexing starts with the
constant-function eigenvalue `0`, and whether the result bounds an individual eigenvalue, a mean,
or a counting/Riesz expression. It must also fix the Euclidean dimension, positivity conditions,
volume normalization, unit-ball constant, and every exceptional low-index case.

The current Chinese gloss, `第一特征值的上界` (upper bound for the first eigenvalue), cannot decide
these matters. In the Neumann convention the first eigenvalue can mean the trivial zero eigenvalue
or the first positive eigenvalue. No formula may be inferred from that gloss.

## Explicit exclusions

- Dirichlet eigenvalue bounds, lower bounds, or asymptotic Weyl law as substitutes.
- The Szego-Weinberger sharp bound for the first nonzero Neumann eigenvalue unless the primary
  source explicitly identifies it as the intended Kroger result.
- A theorem for manifolds or weighted operators in place of bounded Euclidean domains.
- An abstract spectral package that assumes the desired inequality as data.
