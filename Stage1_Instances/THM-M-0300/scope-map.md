# Scope map

## Received result family

The repository phrase can support only this provisional family description:

- a source-selected Hardy space denoted `H^1` on a source-selected domain and measure;
- a source-selected atom predicate, including localization, measurability, size, and cancellation;
- representation of each `H^1` element by a countable summable combination of atoms, in the exact
  convergence and equality sense stated by the source;
- the converse inclusion and quantitative comparison with an infimum of coefficient `l^1` sums
  only if the reviewed source includes those conclusions.

This family description is not a canonical statement and receives no statement or proof credit.

## Proposition-changing choices

An exact source and independent review must determine:

1. Whether `H^1` is the real-variable space on `R^n`, an analytic or boundary Hardy space, or a
   space over another metric-measure structure.
2. Dimension, domain, measure, real or complex scalars, function representatives, and universes.
3. The defining Hardy norm or equivalent model: maximal, Riesz-transform, square-function,
   distributional, boundary-value, or another source-proved definition.
4. Whether atoms are supported on balls or cubes; their measurability and support convention;
   `L-infinity`, `L^2`, or `L^q` size normalization; and mean-zero or higher-moment cancellation.
5. Coefficient type and indexing; finite versus countable sums; absolute summability; and the
   topology or almost-everywhere/distributional sense in which the series converges.
6. Whether the theorem states only decomposition of existing `H^1` elements, both inclusions,
   equality or equivalence of spaces, density, and one-sided or two-sided norm estimates.
7. Exact or existential constants and their dependence on dimension, atom parameters, domain,
   normalization, and scalar field.
8. Ordered binders, hypotheses, conclusion packaging, and all foundation, TCB, and computation
   policies for the selected Lean expression.

No ordered binder list is frozen because these choices yield inequivalent propositions.

## Boundary and degenerate cases

- dimension zero and empty or null-measure domains;
- the zero `H^1` element and zero coefficients;
- zero-radius, empty, or null atom supports;
- equality of functions versus equality almost everywhere or as distributions;
- real versus complex atoms and conjugation conventions;
- finite decompositions versus countable series;
- endpoint atom exponents and alternate cancellation orders;
- convergence in `L^1`, the Hardy norm, almost everywhere, weakly, or distributionally;
- nonunique decompositions and whether an infimum coefficient norm is attained.

No case is silently excluded. The statement phase must incorporate or reject each case through an
approved source mapping and later mutation tests.

## Prohibited substitutions

- Abstract types called `H1` and `Atom` with the requested decomposition assumed as a field.
- Defining `H1` to be atomic combinations and returning the defining witness tautologically.
- Finite-span density in place of a countable atomic representation.
- An `L^1` decomposition that omits the defining Hardy-space condition.
- Only a special dimension, finite model, one atom, one function, or bounded-domain toy result.
- Holomorphic, martingale, operator-algebra, Banach-frame, Besov, or Triebel-Lizorkin atomic
  decompositions substituted for the unidentified catalog target.
- BMO duality, John-Nirenberg, Fefferman-Stein characterization, or a generic integration,
  convergence, `Lp`, Haar-measure, or summability API used as the requested root.
- Any `THM-M-0362` artifact, status, source decision, or future proof used without an accepted
  duplicate-identity decision and exact-statement transport.
- The catalog's `verified` label, a bibliographic lead, or the intake probe used as evidence of a
  human proof, exact formal statement, or machine proof.
