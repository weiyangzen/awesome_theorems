compact sets are compact, and continuous images of compact sets are compact. It attributes the

and reports their current axiom dependencies. The probe does not select their conjunction as the
canonical target, inspect terminal proof provenance, or transfer upstream proof credit. In
particular, intake leaves the exact root null until an admitted source and the statement phase
