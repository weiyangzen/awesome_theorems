# Scope map

## Preserved theorem family

The intake preserves the local center-manifold and nonhyperbolic-equilibrium reduction family named
by the catalog. A later statement phase may select an exact root only from an immutable,
independently reviewed source passage. Candidate components, none yet credited as the theorem, are:

- an autonomous or nonautonomous differential equation near a specified equilibrium;
- a finite-dimensional Euclidean space, Banach space, or another source-selected state space;
- a linearization with a source-defined center/stable/unstable spectral splitting;
- a local center manifold tangent to the center subspace, often represented as a graph;
- a source-specified regularity, locality, invariance, and graph-normalization conclusion;
- a reduced equation or local trajectory correspondence on that manifold; and
- a source-specified stability, instability, asymptotic tracking, or approximation consequence.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

1. Select and preserve one complete source edition, numbered result, incorporated definitions,
   proof boundary, corrections or errata, and independent review.
2. Decide whether the root is existence, local invariance, reduction, stability transfer,
   approximation, parameter dependence, or an explicit conjunction of source clauses.
3. Fix the system form: autonomous ODE, parameter-extended system, semiflow, map, PDE/semigroup, or
   another source model. The catalog category alone does not settle this.
4. Fix the scalar field, state space, dimension or completeness assumptions, vector field, domain,
   equilibrium, neighborhood, and solution notion.
5. State the exact spectral hypothesis and splitting: eigenvalue versus spectrum conventions,
   algebraic multiplicities, generalized eigenspaces, exponential trichotomy, and whether unstable
   directions are present.
6. State every differentiability, Lipschitz, boundedness, vanishing, and derivative-at-equilibrium
   assumption, including the exact regularity inherited by the manifold.
7. Fix whether the manifold is a graph, embedded submanifold, invariant set, or germ; its tangent
   relation; graph value and derivative at zero; and the size and openness of its domain.
8. Resolve local invariance semantics, including exit times, cutoff modifications, forward versus
   two-sided solutions, and whether the conclusion concerns all sufficiently small trajectories.
9. Resolve nonuniqueness: existential choice, equality as germs, finite-order agreement, or a
   source-defined distinguished manifold.
10. Freeze the exact reduced dynamics and the logical strength of any stability or asymptotic
    equivalence transferred between the full and reduced systems.

## Degenerate and boundary cases

Source review must explicitly dispose of a zero-dimensional center subspace; no stable or no
unstable directions; a zero-dimensional state space; a purely center spectrum; the zero nonlinear
term; a nonisolated equilibrium; zero-radius neighborhoods; trajectories leaving the local chart;
eigenvalues on the imaginary axis with nontrivial Jordan blocks; parameter directions introduced
as zero eigenvalues; multiple local center manifolds; finite versus infinite smoothness; and a
reduced equation whose linear or nonlinear terms vanish. These cases affect the theorem's content,
not merely notation.

## Neighbor and substitution exclusions

- `THM-M-1345` Hartman-Grobman and `THM-M-1346` stable-manifold results concern different spectral
  regimes and cannot replace the nonhyperbolic reduction target.
- The separate physics record `THM-P-0741`, attributed to Kelley/Pugh in 1967, has distinct source
  provenance and cannot be merged into this mathematical record.
- A center subspace, invariant linear submodule, ODE existence theorem, or generic flow-invariant
  set alone is not a center-manifold theorem.
- A structure that assumes the desired manifold, invariance, reduction, or stability result as a
  field and then projects it is not a proof.
- A scalar normal-form example, equilibrium-only example, numerical phase portrait, or formal
  special case cannot substitute for a broader source theorem without checked transports.
- The catalog label `verified` and the API probe supply no human-source or machine-proof credit.

No canonical Lean target, expression fingerprint, checked alternate encoding, discovery protocol,
obligation registry, or proof state is frozen at intake.
