## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must verify a primary edition and freeze: connectedness, completeness,
dimension and boundary assumptions; the basepoint and geodesic-ball volume; limsup versus liminf
and normalization of exponential volume growth; finite/infinite volume cases; the sign convention
and self-adjoint realization of the Laplace-Beltrami operator; the definition of the bottom of the
essential spectrum; the precise inequality and constants; and all exceptional or degenerate cases.
It must also determine whether the published theorem has multiple directions or auxiliary
hypotheses that the repository phrase suppresses.
