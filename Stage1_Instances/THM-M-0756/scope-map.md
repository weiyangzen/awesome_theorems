# Scope map

## Preserved topic boundary

The source label concerns hyperarithmetic sets under a source-selected definition or theorem. A
later source and statement freeze must make all of the following explicit:

- the classified objects: subsets or predicates on `Nat`, relations, coded functions, reals, or
  another source-defined domain;
- the base effective operations and whether the presentation is by iterated jumps, effective
  transfinite recursion, definability, relative computability, or checked equivalences among them;
- the recursive-ordinal or notation system, well-foundedness evidence, successor and limit rules,
  and the exact upper boundary;
- the lightface or boldface convention, allowed number or real parameters, standard-model
  semantics, and tuple, formula, tree, or function coding;
- whether equality is extensional equality of sets/functions or equality/equivalence of codes; and
- the exact truth-valued conclusion and every required hypothesis.

This inventory preserves a topic family. It is not a canonical proposition, Lean target, or proof
architecture.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

1. Acquire and independently review an immutable primary or approved authoritative source, with
   exact edition, theorem or definition and page/section, incorporated definitions, assumptions,
   conclusion, proof boundary, corrections, errata, and translation state.
2. Decide whether the scheduled root is a definition package or a particular theorem. A subject
   label or definition alone cannot be reported as a proved proposition merely because the catalog
   treats each row as a theorem candidate.
3. If a theorem is selected, freeze its exact direction and strength. A hierarchy construction,
   characterization, closure result, equivalence of presentations, boundedness theorem,
   completeness result, or properness result cannot stand in for another.
4. Fix the object domain, notation/coding system, computability convention, ordinal boundary,
   successor and limit rules, parameter policy, and all extensionality conventions.
5. Freeze ordered binders, hypotheses, conclusion, foundation and TCB profiles, minimal imports,
   expression/environment fingerprints, alternate encodings, checked transports, and all four
   required statement mutations.
6. Resolve boundary cases including empty/full and computable sets, the base stage, limit stages,
   invalid or non-well-founded codes, parameter-free versus parameter-allowing definitions, and
   sets versus predicates, functions, relations, or reals.

## Explicit exclusions

- Choosing the familiar characterization by a lightface descriptive-set-theoretic class without a
  source decision.
- Choosing closure under effective transfinite recursion, hierarchy strictness, boundedness,
  basis, separation, completeness, or another standard result without a source decision.
- Replacing the target with the arithmetical hierarchy, analytical hierarchy, Turing degrees,
  admissible ordinals, or alpha-recursion theory.
- Presenting ordinary partial recursion, oracle recursion, Turing reducibility, the halting
  theorem, or general well-founded recursion as the root rather than possible infrastructure.
- Treating an arbitrary well-founded relation as an effective recursive-ordinal notation system
  without the source-required encoding and computability evidence.
- Storing the desired characterization in a structure field or hypothesis, or introducing an
  axiom/oracle that assumes it.
- Crediting a finite-stage example, bounded search, catalog status, theorem name, unpinned external
  project, placeholder, adjacent API, or no-match search as source or proof evidence.

No canonical statement, formal expression, alternate transport, obligation registry, discovery
protocol, accepted proof state, or completion claim is frozen at intake.
