## Proof architecture

The selected native route is dyadic chaining. Joint Gaussianity and centering must first yield the
exact increment MGF estimate at the canonical pseudometric scale. Total boundedness and the custom
least-cardinality open-ball covering number then supply finite nets. Parent maps telescope dense
indices through those nets. A finite sub-Gaussian maximum lemma controls each scale; an exact
sum-to-integral comparison must retain the frozen endpoint and constant 12. Finally, a genuine
separability/limit argument must establish both integrability and the numerical inequality.

The external `SLT.Dudley.dudley` theorem is only a provenance overlay. Its sub-Gaussian premise,
closed-ball `WithTop` covering numbers, continuity assumptions, pins, constant, and missing
integrability conjunct prevent it from closing any terminal node without explicit checked bridges.
