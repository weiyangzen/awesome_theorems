# Scope map

## Included theorem family

- A mathematically explicit bulk model, boundary model, and bulk-to-boundary dictionary in a fixed
  regime, rather than an unrestricted claim about all quantum gravity.
- A source-backed consequence with typed observables and equality/equivalence notion. Candidate
  branches are boundary determination of bulk observables, code-subspace reconstruction, or a
  precisely normalized entropy/area relation.
- Every spacetime, dimension, asymptotic condition, state/observable class, coupling or semiclassical
  limit, regularity hypothesis, and units convention used by the selected source result.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must choose exactly one primary-source theorem-level claim. It must fix whether
the target is AdS/CFT, an entropy bound, or code-subspace reconstruction; identify the bulk and
boundary categories and maps; state the relevant notion of equality; expose all approximations and
limits; and specify degenerate cases. Universes, binder order, scalar fields, topology, operator
domains, and any use of finite dimensionality or completion must then be frozen.

The legacy `S1_M_181.lean` definitions may inform API discovery, but its proposition fields and
left-inverse field package conclusions as input data. They are excluded from proof credit and may
not determine the canonical claim without a source crosswalk.

## Explicit exclusions

- The slogan that all bulk information "is encoded" on a boundary without a model and typed map.
- Assuming correlator equality, reconstruction, or an entropy-area inequality as a structure field
  and then projecting that field as the claimed theorem.
- Substituting a generic continuous-linear-map left inverse, spectral identity, or elementary real
  inequality for a quantum-gravity existence/reconstruction result.
- Combining claims from distinct sources or physical regimes into one stronger theorem.
- Treating conjectural AdS/CFT evidence or a toy model as proof of the universal principle.

Until source selection resolves these choices, exact canonical elaboration is deliberately open.
