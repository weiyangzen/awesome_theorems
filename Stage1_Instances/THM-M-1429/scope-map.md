# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

The repository fixes only the Chinese label `Fatou集`, the gloss `复动力学的稳定集` ("the stable
set of complex dynamics"), the attribution Pierre Fatou, the year 1917, and an untrusted `已验证`
status. It supplies no primary source, theorem locator, definition, premise, or conclusion. The
intake therefore preserves only a broad complex-dynamics/Fatou-set topic boundary.

## Proposition-changing decisions

An approved statement correction must obtain an exact source and freeze all of the following:

- the ambient space, such as `ℂ`, the Riemann sphere, or a source-specified Riemann surface, with
  its topology, uniformity, metric, and treatment of infinity;
- the iterated self-map and its class: polynomial, rational, entire, meromorphic, or another
  source-specified map, including degree, nonconstancy, domain, codomain, poles, and singularities;
- whether dynamics is generated by one map, a semigroup, or a family, and exactly which iterates
  form the family being tested;
- the stability predicate: normality in the compact-open or spherical topology, local
  equicontinuity, subsequential local-uniform convergence, or another source-defined notion;
- whether the set is defined pointwise by existence of a stable neighborhood and how convergence
  to infinity, constant limits, meromorphic limits, and compact divergence are treated;
- one truth-valued conclusion, such as openness, forward/backward or complete invariance,
  complementarity with a source-defined Julia set, a component theorem, or a basin result; and
- every ordered binder, premise, exceptional case, and boundary condition needed by that
  conclusion.

These choices yield inequivalent propositions. They are a resolution checklist, not a canonical
claim.

## Candidate families not credited

- A definition of the Fatou set for iterates of a rational map on the Riemann sphere.
- Equivalence between a normal-family definition and local equicontinuity in a spherical metric.
- Openness of a source-specified Fatou set.
- Forward, backward, or complete invariance under the selected map.
- Equality of the Fatou set with the complement of a source-specified Julia set.
- A theorem identifying attracting or parabolic basins, rotation domains, or wandering domains as
  Fatou components.
- A polynomial- or entire-function variant on the complex plane.

No family in this list is selected or credited at intake.

## Explicit exclusions

The intake must not silently replace this target with the separately cataloged general complex
dynamics topic (`THM-M-1427`), Julia set (`THM-M-1428`), Mandelbrot set (`THM-M-1430`), or later
Douady-Hubbard, Yoccoz, Brjuno, Sullivan, McMullen, and renormalization targets. In particular, the
Mandelbrot set is a parameter-space object, not the Fatou set of one dynamical map.

Also excluded are measure-theoretic Fatou's lemma, algebraic-geometry rational maps, a generic
iteration identity, a normal-family or equicontinuity definition with no source-selected theorem,
a tautological theorem that unfolds an invented definition, a constant or identity-map toy case,
and a structure that assumes the desired property as a field. A plotted orbit, numerical fractal,
or unchecked dynamics computation supplies no theorem evidence.

## Degenerate and boundary scope

An exact source must decide constant and degree-zero/one maps, the identity and finite-order maps,
empty or universal Fatou sets, poles and infinity, points where an iterate is undefined in a plane
encoding, finite versus spherical distance, sequences and subnets, constant versus meromorphic
subsequential limits, convergence to infinity, compact divergence, and whether neighborhoods are
taken in the plane, sphere, or a relative domain. It must also distinguish a definition from a
theorem proved about the resulting set.

## Formal boundary

No canonical Lean expression is frozen. Pinned mathlib provides generic function iteration,
periodic-point, meromorphic-function, topology, and locally-uniform convergence APIs.
`TendstoLocallyUniformly` is explicitly documented with a convention that is weaker in general
than the usual neighborhoodwise-uniform definition, so it cannot be adopted silently as
normal-family semantics. A bounded topic-name search found no Fatou-set, Julia-set,
complex-dynamics, or normal-family declaration in pinned mathlib; the `Fatou` hits are the
measure-theory lemma and the `RationalMap` hits are algebraic geometry. These facts show possible
substrate only. They are not an anchor audit, statement elaboration, or machine-proof evidence.
