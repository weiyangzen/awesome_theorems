of designs" (`设计的渐近存在性`), a collective twentieth-century attribution, and an untrusted
`已验证` status. It supplies no citation, definition of design, parameter ranges, divisibility

The spelling is a material identity problem. Bounded repository and bibliographic searches found no
combinatorial-design theorem or author named Keighery. Peter Keevash's *The existence of designs*
(arXiv:1401.3665v4) is a strong discovery lead: its introduction defines `(n,q,r,lambda)` designs
and states that, for fixed parameters, the natural divisibility conditions are sufficient apart from
finitely many values of `n`. That source does not establish that the catalog intended Keevash. Its
author, twenty-first-century date, and exact theorem contract disagree with or go beyond fields in
the catalog: its first arXiv version appeared in 2014 rather than the catalog's twentieth century,
and its exact contract is absent. The collective attribution could instead describe the older
Existence Conjecture. The immediately preceding record separately owns Wilson's design-existence
theorem.

Steiner-system special case, or another design family. No canonical human claim or Lean expression
