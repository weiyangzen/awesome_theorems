# Scope map

## Included claim

- Space-time is `R x R^3` and the equation is `u_tt - Delta u + u^5 = 0`.
- The sign is defocusing and the quintic power is energy-critical in dimension three.
- Cauchy data are smooth and satisfy the finite-energy/localization conditions of the selected
  Grillakis source theorem.
- The conclusion is global classical solvability with persistence of smooth regularity.

## Decisions required during statement

Primary-source inspection must freeze the exact data class (including compact support or decay),
solution regularity, uniqueness interval, energy definition, sign convention, and whether the 1990
or 1992 result supplies the canonical theorem. It must also settle all ordered binders, universes,
time-domain conventions, derivatives, boundary-at-infinity conditions, and degenerate zero-data
case before a fingerprint is assigned.

## Explicit exclusions

- General nonlinearities, focusing sign, subcritical or supercritical powers, other dimensions,
  bounded domains, obstacles, or a radial-only substitute.
- Later energy-class well-posedness or scattering results as replacements for the smooth-regularity
  theorem.
- An abstract package that assumes existence, regularity, or the desired conclusion as a field.
- Energy estimates or finite-dimensional/ODE analogues presented as the terminal theorem.

The formal statement must eventually expose concrete functions, derivatives, PDE satisfaction,
initial traces, energy/data hypotheses, global time domain, and regularity conclusion, or record an
exact missing-API blocker.
