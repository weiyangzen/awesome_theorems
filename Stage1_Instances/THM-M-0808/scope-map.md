# Scope map

## Included topic boundary

- A source-specified projective hierarchy on one fixed real or Polish space.
- The hierarchy's base pointclass, projection and complement operations, and indexing convention.
- Lightface or boldface interpretation and the exact policy for real parameters and codes.
- One exact source-stated classification proposition, with all foundation assumptions explicit.

## Ambiguities to resolve at statement freeze

The repository record does not decide among materially different targets such as:

1. The inductive definitions of `Sigma^1_n`, `Pi^1_n`, and `Delta^1_n`.
2. Closure under continuous preimages, countable operations, complements, or projections.
3. The inclusions and dualities between projective levels.
4. Universal-set, normal-form, separation, reduction, uniformization, or scale theorems.
5. Properness or collapse of the hierarchy, whose hypotheses can involve determinacy, choice, or
   independence-sensitive set theory.

The statement phase must locate an immutable source and freeze one proposition. It must specify the
space and coding of reals, boldface/lightface and parameter conventions, base and level numbering,
ordered binders, all assumptions, and one exact conclusion. A hierarchy definition alone must not
be presented as a theorem unless that is what the pinpoint source actually states.

## Explicit exclusions

- The arithmetical, analytical-in-second-order-arithmetic, Borel, or difference hierarchies as
  substitutes for the projective hierarchy.
- Projective geometry or algebraic projective space.
- Analytic-set facts merely because analytic sets form the first projective level.
- A tautology obtained by defining "projective" to include the desired conclusion.
- A conjunction assembled from unrelated textbook properties without a source.
- The repository label `已验证` as evidence of a human or machine proof.

No canonical Lean target is frozen at intake because the source record does not identify one.
