# Statement blocker

## First failed theorem gate

The rev-5.6 exact-statement gate is blocked before Lean target elaboration. The repository gives
only the method name `Galerkin方法` and the gloss `投影方法`. It fixes no spaces, scalar field,
operator or form, trial/test subspaces, exact or discrete equation, assumptions, ordered binders,
conclusion, or boundary cases.

Galerkin orthogonality, orthogonal best approximation, discrete solvability, Cea quasi-optimality,
and approximation-family convergence are tempting but inequivalent substitutions. Pinned
Lax-Milgram and projection APIs make some encodings feasible; they do not decide which theorem the
catalog intended.

## Retry condition

An accountable statement review must select an immutable primary-source result and freeze its
spaces, scalars, bilinear or sesquilinear convention, trial/test spaces, form and functional,
continuity/coercivity/symmetry assumptions, solution equations, exact-arithmetic boundary, ordered
binders, conclusion, constants, and every degenerate case. An independent source reviewer must
approve the crosswalk. The statement phase can then elaborate the exact Lean expression and run the
required mutations.

Until then, `canonical_statement`, `canonical_claim`, the Lean module/expression and fingerprints,
alternate encodings, obligation registry, and discovery protocol remain null. This intake may be
self-tested, but statement, audit, proof, validation, release, `AUDIT-Z`, and `THEOREM-Z` remain
open.
