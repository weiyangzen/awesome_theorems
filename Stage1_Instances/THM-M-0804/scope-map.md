# Scope map

## Included topic boundary

- Fine-structural inner models intended to accommodate a source-specified large-cardinal strength.
- The exact core model, premouse/extender sequence, and iteration strategy selected by the source.
- A concrete source theorem about existence, iterability, comparison, universality, covering, or
  another stated property.
- Every anti-large-cardinal, countability, well-foundedness, and ambient-foundation hypothesis.

## Ambiguities to resolve at statement freeze

The repository record does not decide among materially different targets:

1. existence or definability of a particular core model below a specified large cardinal;
2. iterability of the constructed premouse or extender model;
3. a comparison or universality theorem for iterable premice;
4. a covering/weak-covering consequence, or a consequence for singular cardinals;
5. a historical Jensen-style core model, a later extender model, or a Steel-style core model.

The statement phase must pin one theorem passage and freeze the ambient theory, all ordered
binders, the precise premouse and iteration notions, hypotheses, conclusion, and boundary cases.

## Explicit exclusions

- The constructible universe `L` or a generic inner-model theorem as a substitute for a core model.
- Inner model theory as a whole, determinacy, or descriptive set theory as a substitute.
- A statement postulating an object called `CoreModel` and merely projecting an assumed property.
- Any convenient theorem about `ZFSet`, ordinals, or cardinals absent a checked source crosswalk.
- The repository label `已验证` as human-source or machine-proof evidence.

No canonical Lean target is frozen at intake because the source record does not identify a
proposition.
