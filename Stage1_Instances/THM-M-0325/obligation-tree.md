# THM-M-0325 frozen obligation architecture

Item: `S56-M-0325-OBLIGATION_TREE`.

The registry freezes 15 semantic obligations before proof execution. The route
records a finite-span, correlation-transform, and random-rounding architecture.
It is an objective decomposition, not a claim that the classical proof or its
primary-source normalization has been formalized.

## Typed proof route

```text
M0325-ROOT exact canonical proposition
`-- M0325-T-ASSEMBLE checked conditional composition
    `-- M0325-T-PACKAGE open exact proof package
        |-- M0325-N-FINITE-SPAN finite-dimensional reduction
        |-- M0325-N-GRAM correlation/Gram encoding
        |-- M0325-K-TRANSFORM universal real transform and constant
        |-- M0325-R-RANDOM random rounding construction
        |   `-- M0325-B-MEASURABLE measurability and integrability
        `-- M0325-L-EXPECTATION expectation estimate
            |-- M0325-B-SCALAR pointwise scalar hypothesis
            `-- M0325-B-MEASURABLE finite-sum interchange
```

`M0325-S-DEFINITIONS`, `M0325-S-BOUNDARY`, and `M0325-S-FOUNDATION` are
refinement and trust obligations. `M0325-X-SOURCE` and
`M0325-X-PROVENANCE` are source and provenance boundaries. None is counted as
a mathematical proof premise or terminal proof body.

## Freeze boundary

The minimal open root cut is `M0325-T-PACKAGE`. The checked conditional
assembly proves neither that package nor any analytic child. Each semantic node
has an explicit budget of at most 100 steps, but those budgets are split
thresholds, not closure evidence. Any split, merge, correction, or eligibility
change requires registry version 2 and an append-only delta. This phase supplies
no H0 source acceptance, root closure, audit completion, or theorem completion.
