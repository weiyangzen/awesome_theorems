# Scope map

## Provisional included claim

- An asymptotically flat gravitational setting with a well-defined total mass.
- A black-hole boundary or horizon cross-section with a well-defined area.
- A lower bound on total mass in terms of that area, with units and normalization made explicit.
- Every energy, causality, regularity, completeness, topology, and horizon hypothesis occurring in
  the primary statement ultimately selected.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must determine whether the source claim is spacetime/dynamical or initial-data,
which mass notion is used, whether the area is event-horizon, apparent-horizon, minimal-surface, or
outer-minimizing area, and whether charge or angular momentum occurs. It must also freeze dimension,
signature, units (`G` and `c`), connectedness, multiple-component behavior, time symmetry, energy
condition, asymptotic ends, regularity, equality case, and the direction and squared/unsquared form
of the inequality.

## Explicit exclusions

- Substituting the Riemannian Penrose inequality assigned to `THM-M-1315` without source evidence
  that the two repository records intentionally duplicate the same theorem.
- Schwarzschild equality alone, a numerical black-hole model, or an abstract real-number inequality.
- Charged, angular-momentum, higher-dimensional, asymptotically hyperbolic, or quasi-local variants
  unless the chosen source statement explicitly selects them.
- Treating a conjectural general spacetime statement as proved because a time-symmetric special case
  has a human proof.

The eventual Lean target must model the selected geometric objects and hypotheses rather than take
the desired mass-area bound as a structure field or hypothesis.
