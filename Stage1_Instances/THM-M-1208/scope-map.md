# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Subject: solutions of one or more unspecified dispersive equations.
- Conclusion: a spacetime estimate, conventionally called a Strichartz estimate.
- Attribution and date: Robert Strichartz, 1977, according to repository metadata.
- The metadata does not distinguish the original restriction-theorem route from later wave,
  Schrodinger, abstract propagator, endpoint, or inhomogeneous formulations.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary theorem and freeze the equation or propagator, spatial
dimension and domain, scalar field, solution notion and initial data, Fourier normalization,
admissible exponent equations and excluded endpoints, homogeneous versus Duhamel form, global versus
local time interval, mixed-norm order, Sobolev regularity/derivative loss, forcing dual spaces,
constant dependencies, and all measurability/integrability assumptions. Boundary cases such as zero
data, infinite exponents, low dimensions, and endpoint pairs must be explicitly included or excluded
from the canonical claim.

## Explicit exclusions

- Choosing a free Schrodinger or wave estimate merely because it has a convenient Lean encoding.
- Substituting the distinct Keel-Tao endpoint theorem, which is separately listed as THM-M-1209.
- Treating a generic implication whose hypotheses already contain the desired estimate as a proof.
- Treating Fourier, `Lp`, Sobolev, or measure-theory infrastructure as a terminal Strichartz theorem.
- Treating the repository label `已验证` as primary-source or kernel evidence.

No theorem tree or proof denominator is frozen during intake; those belong to dependent phases after
the exact statement and source are fixed.
