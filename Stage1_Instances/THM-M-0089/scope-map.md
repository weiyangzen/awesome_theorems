# Scope map

## Preserved theorem family

The intake preserves the Peter-Weyl completeness family for compact topological groups. It does not
choose a canonical proposition among these materially different common surfaces:

- uniform density in `C(G, Complex)` of the complex linear span of matrix coefficients of
  finite-dimensional continuous unitary representations;
- density or a Hilbert-basis/orthogonal-direct-sum statement in `L2(G, haar)`;
- decomposition of a left or right regular unitary representation into finite-dimensional
  irreducible representations; and
- corollaries such as finite-dimensional unitary representations separating points.

A later statement phase may select one root only from a complete, immutable, independently reviewed
source passage. Other equivalent formulations need source-faithful, kernel-checked transports.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

1. Select the exact source edition, result and proof boundary; map every incorporated definition,
   hypothesis and conclusion; audit corrections and translation; and obtain independent review.
2. Fix the group assumptions: compact versus compact Hausdorff topological group, continuity and
   measurability structures, second countability or separability, and whether the trivial or empty
   carrier is admitted.
3. Fix the scalar field and representation model: complex linear, unitary, continuous,
   finite-dimensional, irreducible, and bundled or unbundled.
4. Define a matrix coefficient, including the vector/dual-vector or inner-product convention,
   conjugation orientation, coefficient indexing, and the family over which the span is taken.
5. Fix the Haar measure and normalization. For an `L2` statement, fix the `Lp` encoding, equality
   almost everywhere, inner-product convention, and real/complex scalar handling.
6. Fix the topology and conclusion: uniform closure in continuous functions, `L2` closure, an
   orthonormal/Hilbert basis, a direct-sum isomorphism, or another source-selected statement.
7. Decide whether irreducible representatives require a chosen index of isomorphism classes and how
   dimensions, multiplicities, duals, equivalences, and duplicate representatives are handled.
8. Freeze ordered binders, universe levels, all typeclasses, every boundary case, logical profiles,
   and checked transports for every credited alternate encoding.

## Degenerate and boundary cases

Source review must explicitly dispose of the trivial group; empty or subsingleton carriers; the
trivial representation; zero-dimensional representation spaces; zero vectors and zero
coefficients; duplicate isomorphic irreducibles; nonseparable `L2` spaces; normalization of Haar
mass on a compact group; real versus complex representations; nonfaithful representations;
left-versus-right regular actions; and complex conjugation or inverse conventions.

No case is excluded at intake. A structure that stores density, a basis, a direct-sum equivalence,
or the desired decomposition as an input field is circular rather than a proof.

## Neighbor and substitution exclusions

- `THM-M-0090` (Weyl character formula) and `THM-M-0091` (Weyl dimension formula) concern compact
  Lie-group representation formulas, not completeness for arbitrary compact groups.
- Schur's lemma, Maschke's theorem, character orthogonality, complete reducibility, and Haar-measure
  existence or uniqueness are ingredients or adjacent results, not the Peter-Weyl root.
- Stone-Weierstrass or generic density of continuous functions in `Lp` does not show that matrix
  coefficients supply the dense subspace.
- Plancherel or Parseval statements for locally compact abelian groups, Fourier series on a circle,
  spectral theorems, and finite-group representation decompositions are special or different
  results unless an exact checked reduction is part of the selected source proof.
- A result only for finite, abelian, Lie, metrizable, second-countable, or otherwise specialized
  compact groups cannot replace a source claim quantified over a broader compact-group class.
- A theorem name, DOI, `#check`, generic representation/Haar/`Lp` API, numeric experiment, or the
  catalog's `verified` label supplies no source or machine-proof credit.

## Formal boundary

Pinned mathlib exposes `Representation`, `FDRep`, a chosen Haar measure, density of continuous maps
in `Lp` on compact spaces, and generic Hilbert bases. The probe authenticates only these adjacent
interfaces. It does not define the root family of continuous unitary irreducible representations or
their matrix coefficients, prove coefficient orthogonality or density, or decompose a regular
representation. No canonical Lean target, expression fingerprint, checked transport, mutation
suite, or proof body is claimed at intake.
