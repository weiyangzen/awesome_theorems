# Scope map

## Frozen statement scope

- A smooth real manifold or open subset of Euclidean space and smooth real vector fields.
- The pointwise bracket-generating condition: the Lie algebra generated by the relevant vector
  fields spans every tangent space in the domain under consideration.
- The generated family contains the drift and square fields and is closed under arbitrary finite
  binary Lie brackets.

## Decisions deferred beyond statement phase

Primary-source inspection must still check the exact page wording, coefficient regularity assumed
when this condition is applied, any weighted treatment of the drift, and errata. Operator signs,
zeroth-order terms, Sobolev norms, derivative gain, and analytic regularity conclusions belong to
the neighboring THM-M-1259 theorem rather than this condition-valued target.

## Explicit exclusions

- Treating the bracket-generating condition alone as the theorem without an operator and conclusion.
- Elliptic regularity obtained by assuming the original fields themselves span the tangent space.
- The Chow-Rashevskii accessibility theorem, Malliavin density theorem, or a stochastic analogue.
- A finite-dimensional Lie-algebra rank calculation substituted for distributional PDE regularity.
- Any abstract structure that assumes hypoellipticity or a subelliptic estimate as a field.
