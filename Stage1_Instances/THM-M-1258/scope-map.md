# Scope map

## Included claim family

- A smooth real manifold or open subset of Euclidean space and smooth real vector fields.
- A second-order differential operator whose principal part is a sum of squares.
- The pointwise bracket-generating condition: the Lie algebra generated by the relevant vector
  fields spans every tangent space in the domain under consideration.
- The exact regularity consequence stated by the selected primary theorem: distributional
  hypoellipticity and/or its supporting local subelliptic estimate.

## Decisions deferred to statement phase

Primary-source inspection must fix the operator sign, inclusion and role of a drift field, scalar
zeroth-order terms, real versus complex coefficients, domain and compact-support quantifiers,
finite versus unbounded commutator depth, Sobolev norms and derivative gain, and whether the named
target is the qualitative hypoellipticity result or a quantitative estimate. Binder order and all
regularity assumptions must be copied from that selection rather than reconstructed from memory.

## Explicit exclusions

- Treating the bracket-generating condition alone as the theorem without an operator and conclusion.
- Elliptic regularity obtained by assuming the original fields themselves span the tangent space.
- The Chow-Rashevskii accessibility theorem, Malliavin density theorem, or a stochastic analogue.
- A finite-dimensional Lie-algebra rank calculation substituted for distributional PDE regularity.
- Any abstract structure that assumes hypoellipticity or a subelliptic estimate as a field.
