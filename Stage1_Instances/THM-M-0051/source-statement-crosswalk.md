The catalog's sentence is compatible with several inequivalent propositions. For example,
`ι(v) * ι(v) = 0` and `ι(v) * ι(w) + ι(w) * ι(v) = 0` have different binders and conclusions;
Grassmann-Pluecker relations require yet another coordinate and decomposability setup. This
diagnoses ambiguity but does not authorize any candidate as the root.

The name alone is broader still. Some literature uses Grassmann's formula for the subspace-
dimension identity; pinned mathlib has `Submodule.finrank_sup_add_finrank_inf_eq` in
`Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.FiniteDimensional.Lemmas`. A three-dimensional vector triple-product identity
is also called a Grassmann identity; pinned mathlib has `cross_cross`,
`cross_cross_eq_smul_sub_smul`, and `cross_cross_eq_smul_sub_smul'` in
`Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.CrossProduct`. Those candidates have different domains and conclusions. The
catalog's exterior-algebra gloss makes neither a lawful substitution and still fails to select one
of the several exterior-algebra readings.
