# Scope map

The manifest label means the weighted interpolation inequality family introduced by Caffarelli,
Kohn, and Nirenberg, not their unrelated Navier-Stokes partial-regularity theorem.

## Included claim structure

1. Choose a positive Euclidean dimension and parameters in the primary theorem's admissible region.
2. Impose the dimensional scaling relation linking `p`, `q`, `r`, the three weights, and the
   interpolation coefficient.
3. Quantify over the source-prescribed smooth compactly supported test-function class.
4. Assert existence of a parameter-dependent finite constant.
5. Bound the weighted target norm by the product of weighted gradient and function norms with the
   prescribed interpolation powers.

The source's parameter region is mathematically substantive, not clerical boilerplate. It includes
endpoint and local-integrability restrictions and cannot be reconstructed from scaling alone.
Consequently intake deliberately leaves the Lean expression open rather than inventing a broadened
statement.

## Phase boundaries

The dependent statement phase must inspect the primary theorem, freeze all cases and conventions,
select mathlib representations for test functions, weights, norms, and real powers, elaborate the
target, and mutation-test every side condition. Anchor audit must then search pinned mathlib and
external Lean sources. Obligation-tree, proof, validation, and release work are all downstream and
receive no credit from this dossier.
