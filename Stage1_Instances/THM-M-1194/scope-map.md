# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Named family: Hamilton estimate.
- Attribution and date: Richard Hamilton, 1993.
- Descriptive claim: a matrix Li-Yau inequality.
- Broad subject: differential equations / partial differential equations.

This is all the mathematical scope fixed by the repository sources. In particular, the word
"matrix" suggests a bilinear-form or Hessian comparison, but does not itself fix one.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary source and freeze the paper edition, theorem number and
page; the manifold and metric evolution; dimension, completeness, compactness and time interval;
the positive solution and its PDE; curvature and parallel-curvature hypotheses; sign conventions
for Laplacian, Hessian, curvature and Ricci tensors; the exact pointwise tensor inequality and all
constants; regularity and boundedness assumptions; and endpoint behavior at time zero. It must also
freeze ordered binders, universes, scalar field, foundation/TCB/computation profiles, and degenerate
cases justified by that source.

## Explicit exclusions

- The scalar Li-Yau gradient estimate in place of the matrix inequality.
- A Hamilton differential Harnack estimate for Ricci flow chosen only because it has a nearby name.
- A Euclidean Hessian inequality, maximum principle, or abstract structure that assumes the desired
  matrix inequality as a field.
- The metadata label `已验证` as evidence of either a human proof crosswalk or kernel closure.
- Any mathlib theorem selected before the primary-source claim and assumptions are frozen.
