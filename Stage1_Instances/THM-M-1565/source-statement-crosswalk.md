## Statement and machine boundary

The paper contains many non-equivalent exact results. Choosing the reconstruction theorem produces
a foundational analytic target; choosing an SPDE application produces a much larger probabilistic
target. Intake does not silently identify them. The statement phase must record and independently
review the scope decision, pin the edition, map every binder/premise/conclusion and incorporated
definition, and check corrections before it can claim exact source fidelity.

A scoped local search found no Lean declaration implementing Hairer's regularity structure or
reconstruction theorem. Pinned mathlib mentions Hairer only in bibliography for Gaussian/Fernique
material and contains no matching regularity-structures API. The repository's
`AwesomeTheorems/Stage1/S1_M_182.lean` contains audit/interface material for the separate
`THM-M-1566` target, not a terminal proof of this target. These are intake discovery observations,
not the later immutable anchor audit and not machine-proof evidence.
