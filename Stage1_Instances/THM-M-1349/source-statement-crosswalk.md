The classical criterion has inequivalent variants. A simply connected-domain theorem excluding all
closed trajectories is not identical to multiply connected variants bounding the number of cycles.
Likewise, strict pointwise sign, weak sign with a nonvanishing clause, and almost-everywhere sign
conditions have different hypotheses. The dossier does not choose among them.

Human-source status is therefore `H4`. Required follow-up is to identify a stable primary or
authoritative modern source, record edition and content hash, pinpoint theorem/page and definitions,
transcribe every ordered assumption and conclusion, search errata, and obtain independent review.
Only then may the statement phase choose binders, domain and regularity structures, periodic-orbit
conventions, boundary cases, and checked alternate formulations. No source URL is presented as an
immutable receipt, and the repository label `已验证` is not interpreted as machine verification.
