## Formal source candidate not credited

Bentkamp, first published November 10, 2016, is a published Isabelle/HOL formal development. The
inspected dated release `afp-Deep_Learning-2026-02-06.tar.gz` has SHA-256
`018557d0041584239d603a7eb3700d07ed7eb2a2ca48f694820072003ebf430d` and contains
`DL_Fundamental_Theorem_Network_Capacity.thy`. Within locale
`deep_model_correct_params_y`, theorem `fundamental_theorem_network_capacity_v3` defines a set of
deep-model weights for which a smaller shallow model agrees on every size-compatible input and
proves that set belongs to the null sets of the completed Lebesgue measure.

The AFP development explicitly formalizes and generalizes the mathematics of Nadav Cohen, Or
Sharir, and Amnon Shashua, *On the Expressive Power of Deep Learning: A Tensor Analysis*, COLT
2016, PMLR 49:698-728. The inspected primary paper's Theorem 1 states an almost-everywhere lower
bound on CP rank for tensors generated by a specific hierarchical decomposition; Corollary 2
translates that bound into a deep-versus-shallow network-capacity result under its full assumptions.
