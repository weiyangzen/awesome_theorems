# Scope map

## Source-bounded scope

The only repository claim is the catalog gloss "local and frequency analysis of PDEs". It indicates
the microlocal-analysis domain: simultaneous localization in base variables and cotangent/frequency
variables, commonly expressed using symbols, pseudodifferential operators, or wavefront sets.
It does not specify a predicate, quantifiers, hypotheses, or conclusion.

## Candidate theorem families requiring a source decision

- Characterization of smoothness by absence of wavefront directions.
- Functorial properties of wavefront sets under multiplication, pullback, or pushforward.
- Microlocal elliptic regularity, relating `WF(u)` and `WF(Pu)` to the characteristic set of `P`.

These families are discovery alternatives, not interchangeable formulations. None is selected by
this intake, because selecting one from the field name would broaden or substitute the source.

## Required statement decisions

The statement phase must obtain an authoritative theorem anchor and freeze the distribution space,
base manifold or Euclidean domain, cotangent bundle and deleted zero section, wavefront-set
definition, operator/symbol class, regularity scale, scalar field, all side conditions, binder
order, degenerate cases, and exact conclusion. Only then can universes, imports, foundation,
computation, and trust profiles be fixed.

## Explicit exclusions

- Treating the existence or usefulness of microlocal analysis as a theorem.
- Substituting Fourier inversion, generic PDE regularity, propagation of singularities, or a
  pseudodifferential-operator result without source evidence.
- Encoding the desired conclusion as an assumption, structure field, axiom, or opaque placeholder.
- Crediting the catalog's untrusted "verified" label as source or kernel evidence.
