# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Subject: solutions of an unspecified differential or partial differential equation.
- Claimed property: a bound involving an `L^p` norm of the solution.
- Historical scope: twentieth-century work attributed only to multiple mathematicians.
- Formal status: the repository label `已验证` is untrusted metadata, not proof evidence.

Nothing in the source fixes whether the estimate is a priori or a posteriori, local or global,
elliptic or parabolic, homogeneous or inhomogeneous. This intake does not add those choices.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary theorem and freeze the operator and equation, ambient
domain and measure, scalar/codomain, weak or classical solution notion, boundary and initial
conditions, coefficient and domain regularity, data spaces, exponent range, exact left- and
right-hand norms, constant and its dependencies, and local/global scope. It must explicitly handle
`p = 1`, `p = infinity`, zero or infinite measure, zero data, empty domains, and any dimensional or
compatibility restrictions relevant to the selected source theorem.

## Explicit exclusions

- A generic consequence of `MemLp`, Holder, Minkowski, or norm monotonicity with no PDE content.
- A Calderon-Zygmund second-derivative estimate, heat-equation estimate, Sobolev embedding, energy
  estimate, or maximum principle selected without source provenance.
- Treating the adjacent THM-M-1171 or THM-M-1190 claims as aliases of this target.
- Treating `已验证`, metadata screening, or a library search hit as H0 or M0 evidence.
