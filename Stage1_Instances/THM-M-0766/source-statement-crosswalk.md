## Inspected source leads

S.-Y. Kuroda, *Classes of Languages and Linear-Bounded Automata*, **Information and Control** 7(2)
(June 1964), 207-223, DOI `10.1016/S0019-9958(64)90120-2`, is the primary candidate for the
nondeterministic equivalence. The bibliographic record and DBLP locator were inspected, but the
complete paper, incorporated definitions, theorem locator, proof boundary, and errata were not.

A contemporary review by Richard Stanley, **Journal of Symbolic Logic** 32(1) (1967), page 116,
DOI `10.2307/2271265`, provides a publisher-hosted page-116 preview. It reports that Landweber's
1963 Theorem 3 constructs a type-1 grammar from a deterministic LBA accepting the language, that
the converse was then unknown for that formulation, and that Kuroda proved both directions after
allowing nondeterministic LBAs. The direct preview image hash is recorded in the provisional
receipt. The next review on pages 116-117, DOI `10.2307/2271266`, is Landweber's review of Kuroda.
These are valuable contemporary disambiguation leads, not accepted H0 source crosswalks.

Peter S. Landweber, *Three Theorems on Phrase Structure Grammars of Type 1*, **Information and
Control** 6(2) (1963), 131-136, DOI `10.1016/S0019-9958(63)90169-4`, is the primary lead for the
one-way result. Its complete text was not inspected.

The bounded bibliographic audit found no matching joint Ginsburg/Greibach 1963 work. Their relevant
joint paper, *Mappings which Preserve Context Sensitive Languages*, appeared in 1966 (volume 9(6),
563-582, DOI `10.1016/S0019-9958(66)80016-5`) and names a different family of results. This makes
the catalog attribution a correction blocker rather than source support.
