# Scope map

## Included source family

- Local well-posedness for a nonlinear wave or closely related nonlinear Klein-Gordon Cauchy
  problem, attributed to Jean Ginibre and Giorgio Velo and dated 1985 by repository metadata.
- A concrete theorem specifying spatial dimension, equation and sign, nonlinearity assumptions,
  initial-data spaces, solution space, uniqueness class, lifespan, and continuous dependence.
- Bibliographic searches in the statement phase must distinguish NLW from Ginibre-Velo's nearby
  nonlinear Klein-Gordon work; attribution and year alone do not select a theorem.

## Statement-phase decisions

Freeze the primary-source edition and theorem number, exact operator and mass term, dimension,
nonlinearity and exponent range, data regularity, time interval, solution notion, uniqueness,
continuous dependence, and any smallness or symmetry assumptions. Only then assign ordered Lean
binders, analytic encodings, imports, environment fingerprint, and exact-statement hash.

## Explicit exclusions

- The distinct Segal local-well-posedness target `THM-M-1221` and later critical/global NLW targets.
- Replacing wave by Klein-Gordon, or conversely deleting a mass term, without source justification.
- Substituting global existence, scattering, regularity, an energy estimate, or a Strichartz bound.
- Encoding the desired solution and its properties as assumed structure fields.
- Suppressing dimensional, exponent, sign, regularity, or uniqueness restrictions.
