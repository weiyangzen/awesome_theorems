# Scope map

## Included subject

- An arithmetic surface (a regular arithmetic variety of relative dimension one, subject to the
  selected source's conventions).
- Arakelov intersection theory, metrized/Hermitian line bundles, and a height or intersection
  inequality genuinely stated in the selected Zhang source.
- Every geometric, positivity, generic-fibre, normalization, and characteristic hypothesis used
  by that theorem.

## Statement-phase decisions

The repository label does not determine whether the intended result is Zhang's fundamental
inequality for successive minima and height, an arithmetic intersection positivity inequality, or
another surface specialization. The next phase must inspect the source, quote the exact formula,
and freeze base, regularity, semistability, ampleness/nefness, metric, degree, and normalization
conventions. It must also resolve equality and degenerate cases and binder order.

## Explicit exclusions

- Substituting the classical Arakelov inequality for families of curves or vector bundles.
- Substituting Zhang's small-points or equidistribution theorem.
- Replacing arithmetic surfaces and heights by abstract real variables satisfying an assumed
  inequality.
- Treating the manifest's untrusted `已验证` label as machine or source evidence.
