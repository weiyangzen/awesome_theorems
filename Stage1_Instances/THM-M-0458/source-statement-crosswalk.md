# Source-statement crosswalk

## Primary-source candidate

Shou-Wu Zhang, "Positive line bundles on arithmetic varieties", *Journal of the American
Mathematical Society* 8 (1995), 187-221, is a primary candidate because it develops positivity,
heights, and successive minima for arithmetic varieties. This intake has not inspected and fixed
an exact theorem/page from a stable copy, and therefore does not claim that the repository's name
denotes any particular result in that paper. Suren Arakelov's foundational arithmetic intersection
work is historical context, not yet a statement anchor.

These bibliographic leads are discovery evidence only, not `H0` evidence.

## Acceptance requirement

Before statement credit, a stable primary-source edition must be inspected for theorem number/page,
exact domains, quantifiers, hypotheses, constants, normalizations, and errata. An independent
reviewer must confirm that the chosen result is what the repository label and gloss intend. If the
label remains non-unique, the statement node must remain blocked rather than inventing a theorem.
