# Scope map

## Preserved theorem family

The intake preserves Peixoto's structural-stability results for flows generated by sufficiently
regular vector fields on two-dimensional manifolds. It does not yet choose a canonical proposition.
Candidate results, none credited as the target, include:

- a characterization of structurally stable surface flows by hyperbolic singular/periodic orbits,
  absence of other recurrence, and absence of saddle connections;
- openness of the structurally stable locus in a selected `C^r` vector-field topology;
- density, residuality, or open-density of that locus under source-specific surface and regularity
  hypotheses; and
- a combined theorem asserting characterization and genericity.

The 1962 bibliographic match and the inspected modern discriminator support this family. They do
not authorize intake to pick one root or conflate its components.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

1. Select an immutable source edition and exact theorem passage; bind the 1963 further remark or
   other corrections, proof boundary, incorporated definitions, translation, and independent
   review.
2. Decide whether the root is characterization, openness, density/genericity, or an explicit
   conjunction, and map each conclusion to a source node.
3. Fix the phase space: compact surface or another two-manifold, connectedness, orientability,
   boundarylessness or boundary-transversality conditions, nonemptiness, and manifold model.
4. Fix the vector-field and flow model, differentiability class `r`, the `C^r` or `C^1` topology on
   the function space, completeness/global-flow requirements, and time orientation.
5. Define structural stability precisely: neighborhood quantifiers, orbit equivalence versus
   topological conjugacy, preservation of orbit orientation, and any small-displacement condition.
6. Define singularities, closed/periodic orbits, hyperbolicity, recurrence/nonwandering behavior,
   saddle points, stable/unstable separatrices, saddle connections, and transversality.
7. If genericity is included, distinguish open, dense, open-dense, and residual, and state the
   topology and ambient space exactly.
8. Freeze ordered binders, universes, hypotheses, conclusion, foundation/TCB profiles, minimal
   imports, checked alternate encodings, and all required statement mutations.

## Degenerate and boundary cases

Source review must explicitly dispose of the empty surface; disconnected surfaces; the disc or a
surface with boundary; orientable versus nonorientable compact surfaces; `r = 1` versus `r >= 2`;
zero vector fields; no singularities; no periodic orbits; nonhyperbolic equilibria or cycles;
homoclinic and heteroclinic saddle connections; recurrent nonperiodic orbits; global versus local
flows; time reparametrization; and whether an orientation-reversing orbit map is permitted.

These choices are material. The inspected modern source says that for compact orientable surfaces
and `r >= 1` the structurally stable locus is open-dense and gives the dynamical characterization,
but separately says that density on nonorientable surfaces for `r >= 2` remains open except for
specified cases, while the `C^1` result does not need orientability. Intake must not erase that
boundary.

## Substitution exclusions

- `THM-M-1366` (general structural stability) and `THM-M-1368` (Morse-Smale systems) are neighboring
  targets, not sources of statement or proof credit for this item.
- A theorem for planar polynomial systems, the two-disc, a single named surface, gradient flows,
  diffeomorphisms, discrete iteration, or higher-dimensional Axiom A systems cannot replace a
  source-selected surface-flow theorem.
- Characterization, openness, and density cannot silently stand in for one another.
- A nonorientable `C^r`, `r >= 2`, density claim cannot be inferred from the orientable theorem.
- Generic flow, integral-curve, omega-limit, homeomorphism, diffeomorphism, compactness, or
  periodic-point APIs are substrate only.
- A structure or hypothesis containing structural stability, hyperbolicity, no recurrence, no
  connections, openness, or density as assumed data cannot count as a proof.
- The catalog's `verified` label, source metadata, modern summary, and API probe provide no machine
  proof credit.

## Formal boundary

Pinned mathlib supplies manifolds and tangent bundles, smooth sections, manifold integral curves,
continuous flows and orbits, homeomorphisms/diffeomorphisms, semiconjugacy, and omega limits. The
intake probe checks representative interfaces only. No exact Peixoto declaration, structural-
stability predicate for vector-field perturbations, hyperbolic periodic-orbit package, recurrence
classification, saddle-connection predicate, or function-space genericity theorem is credited.

The statement phase must first obtain a source-approved proposition, then implement or identify its
definitions, minimize imports, elaborate and fingerprint the exact expression, check transports,
and mutation-test removed hypotheses, domain changes, binder scope, and boundary cases. No
obligation registry or proof graph is frozen at intake.
