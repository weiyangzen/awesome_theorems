# Scope map

## Literal included scope

- The repository subject is Morse-theoretic reasoning applied to a partial differential equation.
- A future exact claim must identify a concrete PDE and the variational/critical-point object whose
  Morse data implies a stated PDE conclusion.
- Only hypotheses and boundary cases present in a selected primary theorem may enter the target.

This is a scope family, not yet a theorem statement. Intake does not choose among finite- or
infinite-dimensional Morse theory, Morse inequalities, critical groups, Morse index estimates,
existence, or multiplicity.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

The statement phase must identify and inspect a primary theorem and freeze: the PDE, domain and
dimension; boundary/initial conditions; function and coefficient spaces; ellipticity or other PDE
class; associated energy functional; differentiability and compactness hypotheses such as
Palais-Smale; nondegeneracy or isolation of critical points; coefficient ring; Morse index and
critical-group conventions; quantifiers; and the exact existence, multiplicity, or inequality
conclusion. It must also settle the zero solution, degenerate critical points, empty critical sets,
noncompact domains, and regularity needed to turn weak critical points into PDE solutions.

## Explicit exclusions

- The finite-dimensional Morse lemma or Morse inequalities alone as a substitute for a PDE result.
- A generic critical-point existence theorem with no checked bridge to a specified PDE.
- An example, survey theme, or method description presented as one theorem.
- A structure or hypothesis that assumes the desired solution or multiplicity conclusion.
- The metadata label `已验证` as human-proof or Lean kernel evidence.
