## Source ambiguity boundary

No primary mathematical source was supplied by the repository records inspected at intake. In
particular, this dossier does **not** silently substitute definability of types, the fundamental
theorem of stability, the finite-equivalence-relation theorem, or any other familiar stability
result. Those may be search leads, but accepting one without a matching source would broaden or
replace the assigned theorem.

To clear the source gate, a later audit must produce an immutable bibliographic edition and pinpoint
theorem/page, quote or faithfully transcribe the statement and definitions, map every hypothesis and
quantifier, check corrections/errata, and obtain independent review. Only then can the statement
phase choose Lean objects, serialize a normalized expression, and mutation-test domains, binders,
hypotheses, and boundary cases.

No `H0` or exact-statement claim is made.
