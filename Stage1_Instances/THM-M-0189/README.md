This directory is the `planned` intake dossier for the classical Minkowski problem for surface-area
measures of convex bodies. It freezes the intended human claim as the existence and translation
uniqueness characterization for a finite Borel measure on the unit sphere, with the zero first
moment and nondegeneracy conditions made explicit.

The repository's Chinese gloss and `已验证` label are discovery metadata only. Exact primary-source
wording, the dimension convention, the relevant notion of surface-area measure, and a Lean 4
encoding remain downstream gates. The provisional root vector is `[H2, M4, R4]`; there is no
accepted proof state, elaborated canonical target, audit completion, or theorem completion.

`scope-map.md` records inclusions and exclusions, `source-statement-crosswalk.md` maps the source
claim to required formal objects, and `task-dag.json` keeps every later phase open. Commands and
results used to self-test this intake are in `validation.md`.
