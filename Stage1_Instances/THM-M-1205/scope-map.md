# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Family: compensated compactness associated with Luc Tartar and Francois Murat.
- Setting: differential equations, with the repository specifically mentioning conservation laws.
- Intended effect: strong compactness or nonlinear weak-limit control obtained from compensating
  differential constraints.
- Date metadata: 1978. This is discovery metadata, not an exact theorem anchor.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must choose a primary theorem and freeze whether the target is a div-curl
lemma, weak continuity of a quadratic form, Young-measure reduction, or an entropy compactness
result. It must then fix the domain and dimension, sequence/function spaces and exponents,
differential constraints, topology of precompactness, boundary/localization assumptions, flux and
entropy regularity, conclusion, binder order, degenerate cases, and foundation profile.

## Explicit exclusions

- Ordinary topological compactness, uniform tightness, or compact operators by themselves.
- A div-curl lemma or a Kruzkov entropy theorem selected solely because it is easier to encode.
- An abstract proposition-valued hypothesis that assumes the terminal compensation mechanism.
- The legacy module's conditional wrappers as proof of compensated compactness.
- The metadata label `已验证` as either human-source or kernel evidence.

The canonical target remains open until a primary-source theorem resolves these alternatives.
