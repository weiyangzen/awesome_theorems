## Proof architecture

turns the arbitrary functional `F` into a vector `f`, and coercivity upgrades
the induced bilinear operator to a continuous linear equivalence. The latter is
expanded through its lower bound, antilipschitz estimate, trivial kernel,
closed range, and orthogonal-complement surjectivity argument. Separate apply,
orientation, and uniqueness nodes prevent the continuous equivalence from
silently standing in for the source-level `exists unique` conclusion.

The proof graph has reciprocal `proof_requires` and `composes` edges. Separate
refinement, provenance, evidence, trust, documentation, and workflow graphs
ensure that a citation, candidate label, or workflow receipt cannot count as a
proof edge. Every node owns a semantic ledger and at most 100 planned steps.
