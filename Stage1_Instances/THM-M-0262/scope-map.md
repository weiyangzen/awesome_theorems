# Scope map

## Preserved catalog scope

The repository fixes only target `THM-M-0262`, the title `沙利定理`, Dennis Sullivan, the year
1985, and the gloss `有理函数动力学的分类` (`classification of rational-function dynamics`).
Importance `high` and status `已验证` are catalog metadata, not a theorem statement or proof
evidence. Intake preserves this historical complex-dynamics boundary without choosing a theorem
from memory.

The word "classification" is mathematically incomplete here. It does not say what is classified,
under which relation, into which classes, or with which invariants. Plausible readings concern
rational maps, their Julia or Fatou components, stable parameter regions, dynamical conjugacy,
quasiconformal deformation, critical-orbit behavior, or another object. These lead to inequivalent
propositions.

## Proposition-changing decisions

An approved source correction must select one exact proposition and freeze:

- an immutable Sullivan or coauthored source, exact theorem or proposition locator, incorporated
  definitions, proof boundary, corrections, errata, and independent source review;
- the phase space: the complex Riemann sphere, projective line, one-point compactification, or an
  explicitly transported equivalent model;
- the dynamical map: an algebraic rational function, a total rational sphere self-map, a polynomial,
  or a parameterized family, including degree, nonconstancy, normalization, poles, and infinity;
- the classified object: maps, conjugacy classes, Julia or Fatou components, parameter-space
  components, stable systems, critical or postcritical data, or another source-defined object;
- the classification relation and output: topological, conformal, quasiconformal, or hybrid
  conjugacy; structural stability; named component types; invariants; finiteness; exhaustiveness;
  or another exact conclusion;
- every quantifier over maps, parameters, points, components, neighborhoods, iterates, and
  deformation families, with all regularity, critical-orbit, and exceptional-map hypotheses; and
- constant and degree-one maps, Mobius and Lattes-type exceptions, empty or whole-sphere invariant
  sets, poles and infinity, parabolic or indifferent cycles, and every other boundary case.

These are resolution requirements, not a canonical statement.

## Candidate families not credited

- Stability and bifurcation results from Mañé-Sad-Sullivan, *On the dynamics of rational maps*.
- Sullivan's quasiconformal-deformation and structural-stability results for rational maps.
- Classification of periodic Fatou components or another Fatou-Julia decomposition theorem.
- Eventual periodicity of Fatou components, equivalently a no-wandering-domain formulation.
- A rigidity, invariant-line-field, hyperbolicity, density, entropy, or parameter-space theorem.
- A classification of one convenient family such as quadratic polynomials.

No family in this list is selected, asserted, or credited at intake.

## Neighbor and substitution exclusions

- `THM-M-1434` separately owns Sullivan's no-wandering-domain theorem. Its source, statement,
  dossier, receipts, and any future proof credit cannot select or close this target.
- `THM-M-1427`, `THM-M-1428`, and `THM-M-1429` separately own the broad complex-dynamics, Julia-set,
  and Fatou-set labels. Their future corrections cannot silently become this root.
- `THM-M-0259`, `THM-M-0260`, and `THM-M-0261` separately own the neighboring McMullen, Yoccoz,
  and Mandelbrot-connectivity entries.
- Generic analytic, meromorphic, rational-function, iterate, connected-component, or periodic-point
  APIs are substrate only. They do not state a Sullivan classification theorem.
- A structure that stores the desired classification, stability, conjugacy, or nonwandering
  conclusion as a field is circular rather than proof evidence.
- A plotted fractal, finite orbit computation, numerical experiment, or unchecked certificate
  cannot select or prove the target.

## Formal boundary

No canonical Lean expression is frozen. Pinned mathlib exposes algebraic rational functions,
one-point compactification, meromorphic predicates, iteration, connected components, and generic
periodic points. A bounded source-name search found no target-specific Sullivan, rational-dynamics,
Julia-set, Fatou-set, or wandering-domain declaration. These are intake discovery facts only, not
an exhaustive anchor audit, exact statement, or machine proof.
