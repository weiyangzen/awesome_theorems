intended human claim as the classical closed-manifold formula: on a compact oriented boundaryless
Riemannian manifold of even dimension, the integral of the convention-normalized Pfaffian of the
Levi-Civita curvature equals the Euler characteristic.

representation of the Euler characteristic," the attribution to Shiing-Shen Chern, and the year
1944. Its `已验证` label is explicitly untrusted metadata. The source crosswalk therefore records
the 1944 paper as a candidate primary anchor but leaves edition-level formula, sign, normalization,
assumption, and errata inspection open. It does not claim `H0`.

variants, characteristic-class restatements without a Chern-Weil bridge, and abstract substitutes.

provisional root vector is `[H1, M4, R4]`; no canonical Lean expression or proof state is accepted.
