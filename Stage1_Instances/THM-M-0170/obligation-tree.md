# THM-M-0170 frozen obligation tree

This registry freezes a classical smooth Nash route before granting closure credit. It separates
the compact construction from the noncompact exhaustion and compatibility problem, while sharing
the free-embedding, defect-decomposition, perturbation, smoothing, and iteration engines. The
pinned mathlib audit found object-model and Whitney substrate, not any of these central packages.

## M0170-S-BOUNDARY

Account explicitly for empty, zero-dimensional, disconnected, and `n = 0` target behavior. No

## M0170-C-FREE

## M0170-L-DEFECT

## M0170-L-PERTURB

Realize a primitive correction by a smooth high-frequency perturbation, with exact derivative and
embedding-control estimates. This central Nash step cannot be hidden behind an analytic slogan.

## M0170-L-SMOOTH

norm, regularity index, interpolation inequality, and parameter dependence used by iteration.

## M0170-C-ITER

and persistence of topological embedding. Approximate or `C^1` closure is not sufficient.

## M0170-L-COMPAT

For the noncompact branch, prove local finiteness and compatibility of exhaustion-stage corrections,
including stability on earlier compact sets and existence of one finite-dimensional target.

## M0170-B-NONCOMPACT

branch packages and yields the exact root; it proves neither branch.

errata. This documentation overlay carries no machine-proof credit.

## M0170-X-PROVENANCE

Track unique terminal bodies, wrappers, dependency closure, axiom reports, replay receipts, and
revocation inputs. It is a release overlay and cannot occur as a proof premise.
