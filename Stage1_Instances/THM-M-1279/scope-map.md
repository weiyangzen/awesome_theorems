# Scope map

## Included claim

- Beckner's sharp higher-dimensional endpoint Sobolev/Moser-Trudinger inequality on a sphere.
- The source's actual sphere dimension, admissible real-valued function space, normalized surface
  measure, mean/normalization constraint, conformally invariant quadratic or higher-order energy,
  exponential term, and sharp constant.
- The endpoint/limiting relationship to the sharp Sobolev family only where the selected primary
  theorem makes it part of the statement or proof architecture.

## Statement-phase decisions

The primary text must fix the exact numbered theorem and determine whether the target is the even-
dimensional exponential inequality, a logarithmic Sobolev formulation, or a parameterized sharp
Sobolev inequality commonly carrying Beckner's name. It must also fix Laplacian sign, sphere and
measure normalization, smooth versus completed Sobolev domain, real/complex scalars, constants,
equality cases, low-dimensional and constant-function cases, and all binder scopes.

## Explicit exclusions

- The Gaussian Beckner interpolation inequality or Beckner's uncertainty principle.
- A generic inequality named after Beckner with a different domain or parameter range.
- The classical two-dimensional Moser-Trudinger or Onofri inequality substituted for the stated
  higher-dimensional result.
- A weakened non-sharp estimate, an assumed analytic package, or an equality-case-only theorem.

The next phase must encode the selected source statement exactly. Until then, formulas are not
guessed and no Lean theorem declaration receives statement credit.
