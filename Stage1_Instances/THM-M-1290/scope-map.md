# Scope map

## Included claim family

- A bounded domain `Omega` in Euclidean space of dimension `n >= 3`, with regularity to be fixed.
- The zero-Dirichlet critical problem `-Delta u = lambda u + u^(2* - 1)`, where
  `2* = 2n/(n-2)`, interpreted through an appropriate positive weak solution in `H_0^1(Omega)`.
- Existence and nonexistence regimes involving the first Dirichlet eigenvalue `lambda_1(Omega)`.
- The dimension-three exception and its domain-dependent or ball-specific threshold only if the
  chosen primary theorem states it.

## Decisions deferred to the statement phase

Primary-source inspection must fix whether the target is the general existence theorem, a
nonexistence theorem, or a conjunction; `Omega`'s smoothness and star-shaped hypotheses; the exact
open or closed bounds on `lambda`; positivity conventions; weak versus classical solutions; and the
normalization of the Laplacian, eigenvalue, norm, and critical power. It must separately decide the
`n >= 4`, general `n = 3`, and three-dimensional ball cases rather than merge their thresholds.

## Explicit exclusions

- A subcritical equation, a whole-space equation, or a generic semilinear PDE existence theorem.
- The Brezis-Lieb lemma, Aubin-Talenti extremals, or the Sobolev inequality as substitutes.
- Assuming a solution, compactness, or a Palais-Smale conclusion in a structure field.
- Replacing the critical exponent by a natural-number polynomial exponent.
- Treating the historical metadata label as either a human-proof or machine-proof receipt.

The formal target must expose the PDE/weak formulation, boundary condition, positivity, parameter
range, and domain hypotheses rather than package the desired result as input data.
