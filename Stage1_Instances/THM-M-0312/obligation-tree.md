## Proof route

The exact root composes two interfaces: pointwise norm boundedness gives uniform equicontinuity,
and uniform equicontinuity gives a common operator-norm bound. The first interface specializes the
singleton norm seminorm family, obtains barrelledness from completeness through the Baire-space
instance, and applies `WithSeminorms.banach_steinhaus`. That terminal body separately owns the
bounded composed-seminorm range and continuity of its pointwise supremum. The second interface uses
the required direction of `NormedSpace.equicontinuous_TFAE`. Statement, boundary, iSup transport,
foundation, source, and provenance nodes stay in separate typed graphs.

Every proof requirement has a reciprocal composition edge. Provenance, trust, documentation, and
workflow relationships cannot close proof nodes. Each semantic leaf has a substantive ledger and a
budget no greater than 100.

explicit premises. The anchor audit located a pinned exact `banach_steinhaus` candidate and its
general barrelled terminal body, but this phase accepts no proof evidence and does not promote the
root to M0-W. Primary-source node mapping, complete declaration provenance, transitive trust, R0,
