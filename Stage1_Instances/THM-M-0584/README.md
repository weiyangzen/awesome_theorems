# THM-M-0584 rev-5.6 intake

This directory is the `planned` intake for the repository entry called "Donaldson theorem" whose
source phrase is "differential structures of four-dimensional manifolds." The phrase most directly
points to Donaldson's intersection-form (diagonalization) theorem, rather than the distinct
ASD-moduli-space family recorded as `THM-M-0184`.

The intended root is therefore provisionally scoped as follows: the integral intersection form of
a closed, oriented, smooth, simply connected, definite four-manifold is diagonalizable over the
integers (with the positive-definite orientation giving the standard positive diagonal form). This
is a theorem family, not yet an accepted exact statement. The source edition, precise compactness
wording, orientation convention, positive/negative formulation, and the definition of equivalence
of forms must be pinned and independently reviewed before the statement phase may freeze binders.

No Lean target or proof is credited. In particular, `THM-M-0184` and its historical
`S1_M_131.lean` artifact concern ASD moduli-space interfaces and cannot substitute for this target.
The provisional root vector is `[H1, M4, R4]`; audit completion and theorem completion are false.
