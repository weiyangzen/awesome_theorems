# Scope map

## Included subject boundary

- Singular homology and cohomology of a topological manifold, with cap product against a
  fundamental class.
- The leading candidate is the closed, connected, orientable `n`-manifold theorem: for each `k`,
  capping with `[M]` identifies degree-`k` cohomology with degree-`n-k` homology.
- Objects that the statement phase must freeze include the manifold category and regularity,
  coefficient ring or local system, orientation datum, compactness, connectedness, dimension,
  homology theory, grading convention, and the direction/sign convention of cap product.
- Boundary cases include `k > n`, `n = 0`, disconnected manifolds, nonorientable manifolds,
  manifolds with boundary, and relative or compactly-supported variants.

## Variant decision

The metadata title and gloss do not uniquely select among closed orientable duality, duality with
local coefficients, Lefschetz duality for a boundary, or compact-support duality for noncompact
manifolds. The statement phase must select one exact source theorem. It must not silently broaden
the closed case or substitute a finite-dimensional vector-space dimension equality for the
cap-product isomorphism.

## Explicit exclusions

- Poincare-Lefschetz, Alexander, Serre, or Verdier duality as a substitute for the named root.
- Euler-characteristic symmetry, equality of Betti numbers, or a low-dimensional example alone.
- A proposition that assumes the desired isomorphism or hides it in an unconstrained predicate.
- Legacy metadata status or an existing API object as proof credit.

The later statement phase owns universes, ordered binders, exact imports and declaration type,
environment fingerprint, checked alternate-form transports, and hypothesis/boundary mutations.
