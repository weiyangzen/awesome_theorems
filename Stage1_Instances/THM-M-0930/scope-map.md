# THM-M-0930 scope map

## Received claim

The repository supplies the name "Combinatorial Nullstellensatz" and the gloss "applications of
the polynomial method in combinatorics." Intake preserves the theorem family identified by that
name, attribution, and year. The gloss describes a method and its uses, not one truth-valued
proposition, so it is not promoted into a canonical statement.

The current ID is also bound to its name and gloss. Before the Stage0 deduplication revision
`c61be3c80710c07c5f7626e3404e51f40ecb39a6`, this record was `THM-M-0957`; the old
`THM-M-0930` named the Bose-Shrikhande-Parker theorem. An old numeric ID alone is not valid source
identity.

## Candidate roots

The inspected primary paper explicitly calls both of the following results Combinatorial
Nullstellensatz:

1. **Alon 1999, Theorem 1.1:** over a field, a polynomial vanishing on a nonempty finite product
   grid belongs to the ideal generated by the univariate grid polynomials, with degree bounds and a
   further subring-preservation clause.
2. **Alon 1999, Theorem 1.2:** over a field, a polynomial whose total degree is the sum of a chosen
   multi-index and whose corresponding coefficient is nonzero has a nonzero evaluation on every
   family of sets whose cardinalities exceed the multi-index coordinates.
3. **A two-result package:** both theorems, perhaps with Lemma 2.1 or specified applications, could
   be intended by the catalog's method gloss.

These are candidate scopes only. Choosing one changes the root and downstream obligation graph.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

An approved source statement and its Lean transport must decide:

1. Theorem 1.1, Theorem 1.2, or an explicit conjunction/package as the root.
2. Field coefficients as in the paper versus mathlib's commutative-domain generalization.
3. A fixed natural number of variables versus a finite variable type, and the exact empty-variable
   convention.
4. Finite sets versus duplicate-free finsets and all nonemptiness or cardinality requirements.
5. Ordinary multivariate-polynomial notation versus `MvPolynomial` with finitely supported
   exponent vectors.
6. For Theorem 1.1, grid vanishing, generator orientation, representation as a finite sum or
   `linearCombination`, the exact degree inequality, and whether the source's subring clause is in
   scope.
7. For Theorem 1.2, total-degree equality, selected coefficient, point-membership conclusion, and
   strict cardinality inequalities.
8. Ordered binders, universe and typeclass assumptions, foundation and TCB profiles, alternate
   encodings, and every checked relationship.

## Boundary cases

Statement review must resolve the empty variable type; empty and singleton coordinate sets; the
zero and constant polynomials; the zero multi-index; coefficient rings with one element; finite
domains; variables unused by the polynomial; exact versus larger grid cardinalities; and whether
Theorem 1.1's nonempty-grid hypothesis or Theorem 1.2's strict inequalities make particular cases
vacuous. No case is excluded at intake.

## Rejected substitutions

- Hilbert's algebraic Nullstellensatz or weak Nullstellensatz.
- Schwartz-Zippel or a probabilistic polynomial identity bound.
- The grid-vanishing lemma alone.
- Alon-Tarsi list-coloring, Cauchy-Davenport, Chevalley-Warning, graph-coloring, additive-number-
  theory, or other applications presented as the root.
- A theorem specialized to one coefficient field, one number of variables, or one application
  unless an accepted source statement requires it.
- A structure or premise that stores the desired decomposition or nonzero evaluation.
- The untrusted catalog label, module documentation, API output, or an axiom diagnostic used as
  proof credit.
