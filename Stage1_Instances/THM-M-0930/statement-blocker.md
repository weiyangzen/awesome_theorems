## Decision

The exact Lean 4 target cannot be truthfully elaborated from the authoritative repository record.
The complete catalog wording is the name Combinatorial Nullstellensatz, the attribution to Noga
Alon in 1999, and the gloss "applications of the polynomial method in combinatorics." That gloss
describes a method and its uses rather than a proposition. Stage0 explicitly leaves the formal
system, exact definitions and assumptions, proof route, equivalent forms, logical principles,
machine status, and formal artifacts open. The catalog's `verified` label is untrusted inventory
metadata under rev-5.6.

The inspected primary paper makes the ambiguity concrete rather than resolving it. Alon states and
proves two different results, then says that both "may be called Combinatorial Nullstellensatz":

1. Theorem 1.1 is a product-grid vanishing theorem. Over a field, it gives a representation in the
   ideal generated by the univariate grid polynomials, with degree bounds and a further
   subring-preservation clause.
2. Theorem 1.2 is a selected-coefficient nonvanishing theorem. An exact total-degree and nonzero
   coefficient hypothesis gives a point with nonzero evaluation in every sufficiently large
   product grid.

These conclusions are not interchangeable, and the catalog selects neither one nor a package
containing both. It also does not decide the source field versus mathlib's commutative-domain
generalization, fixed variables versus a finite variable type, finite-set encoding, degree and
coefficient conventions, Theorem 1.1's subring clause, ordered binders, or boundary cases. Selecting
one familiar pinned declaration would therefore invent, narrow, broaden, or substitute
proposition-changing mathematics.

expression fingerprint hard blockers. The intake correctly leaves the canonical human statement,
