## Energy boundary

The anchor audit established that coordinate energy and operator-norm energy are not definitionally
equal. This registry does not pretend otherwise. `M1244-L-POINTWISE` asks only for the directional
inequality needed by the final transitivity step; its truth and exact constant must be proved for
Lean's `Fin n -> Real` product norm. If that direction fails, the root is blocked rather than
silently changed to the upstream theorem.

Every current leaf has a substantive ledger and a planning budget at most 100. Proof execution must
split any leaf that reveals a hidden theorem package, representation change, case split, or longer
ledger. The threshold is not closure or readability evidence.
