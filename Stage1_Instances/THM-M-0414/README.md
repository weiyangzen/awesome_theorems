Dedekind domain. The metadata claim is frozen at the human level: every nonzero proper ideal of a
Dedekind domain factors uniquely, up to order, as a finite product of nonzero prime ideals.

rev-5.6 statement or proof credit at intake. In particular, the later statement phase must decide
whether the canonical Lean target uses the classical existence-and-uniqueness formulation or a
checked equivalent finite-product/unique-factorization-monoid formulation.

expression, accepted proof state, audit completion, or theorem completion is claimed. Downstream
