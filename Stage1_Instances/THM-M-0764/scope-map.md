# Scope map

## Preserved theorem family

The intake preserves the standard theorem family relating context-free languages and pushdown
automata. The catalog fixes only the title `下推自动机`, the gloss `上下文无关语言的识别`, a
collective attribution, and a twentieth-century period. It supplies no formula, bibliography,
definitions, or proof.

These familiar mathematical shapes are candidates, not adopted statements:

- **Grammar to automaton:** every language generated by a context-free grammar is accepted by a
  source-specified nondeterministic pushdown automaton.
- **Automaton to grammar:** every language accepted by such an automaton is generated by a
  context-free grammar.
- **Class equivalence:** a language is context-free if and only if some source-specified pushdown
  automaton accepts it.
- **Construction correctness:** one concrete CFG-to-PDA or PDA-to-CFG transformation preserves
  word membership.

The outside-Stage1 catalog record `THM-C-0141` explicitly names the biconditional, but it is only a
neighboring discovery lead. No alias or ownership decision maps it to this target.

## Decisions required at statement freeze

An approved source review must freeze all of the following before any candidate becomes canonical:

1. An immutable primary or approved authoritative source edition, exact result locator,
   incorporated definitions and assumptions, proof boundary, corrections and errata, and
   independent review.
2. The root direction: CFG-to-PDA, PDA-to-CFG, both directions as an `Iff`, or correctness of one
   named construction.
3. The terminal alphabet, word representation, language equality convention, and finiteness or
   decidable-equality assumptions.
4. The grammar model: nonterminal carrier and finiteness, production representation, start symbol,
   derivation relation, and generated-language definition.
5. The automaton model: finite control states, initial state, stack alphabet and initial/bottom
   marker, transition relation, nondeterminism, and whether transitions may consume epsilon input
   or push/pop strings rather than one symbol.
6. The acceptance convention: final state, empty stack, both, or a checked equivalence between
   conventions, including end-of-input behavior.
7. Ordered binders, hypotheses, exact conclusion, construction witnesses, equality or extensional
   equivalence, Lean universes, and all typeclass assumptions.
8. Every checked transport among language predicates, grammar encodings, PDA encodings, and
   acceptance conventions, plus the foundation, TCB, and computation policies.

## Boundary cases to resolve

- Empty terminal alphabet, empty word, and empty language.
- Grammars with no productions, useless or unreachable nonterminals, epsilon productions, unit
  productions, and left recursion.
- Empty stack alphabet or control-state type, absent initial state, and explicit bottom markers.
- Transitions that read no input, push or pop nothing, or combine input and stack operations.
- Infinite epsilon-transition paths, stuck configurations, and rejection/halting conventions.
- Acceptance with unread input, acceptance in the initial configuration, and empty-stack versus
  final-state acceptance.
- Whether finite carriers are represented by `Fintype`, explicit `Finset`s, or finite reachable
  support inside a larger type.

No case is excluded before the exact proposition is selected.

## Explicit exclusions

- Treating `Language.IsContextFree`, `ContextFreeGrammar.language`, or closure under reversal as the
  CFG/PDA equivalence theorem.
- Using the deterministic multi-stack `Turing.TM2` model as a standard nondeterministic one-stack
  PDA without a source-matched checked specialization and semantics transport.
- Replacing pushdown automata with finite automata, regular expressions, linear-bounded automata,
  or unrestricted Turing machines.
- Proving only one direction while presenting the biconditional as closed, or silently replacing
  the catalog gloss with the separate `THM-C-0141` record.
- Assuming an acceptance-equivalence field, grammar/automaton conversion, or root theorem inside a
  structure and then presenting that assumption as a proof.
- Treating the catalog's `已验证` label, bibliographic metadata, bounded search, or API probe as
  human-source or machine-proof evidence.

## Lean boundary

Pinned mathlib supplies a substantive CFG-side interface but not the missing PDA side of the
candidate equivalence. The intake therefore records `M3` for statement/interface debt, not root
closure. The general `Turing.TM2` stack-machine interface is adjacent substrate only: its semantics
are deterministic and permit multiple stacks and internal memory. A full immutable formal-candidate
search belongs to `S56-M-0764-ANCHOR_AUDIT` after the exact statement is accepted.
