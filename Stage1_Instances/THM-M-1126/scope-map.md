# Scope map

## Preserved source scope

- Subject: the one-dimensional wave equation and a solution of it.
- Attribution and date: Jean le Rond d'Alembert, 1746, as repository metadata.
- Claimed kind: an unspecified mathematical theorem or proposition about a solution.

Nothing in the available record fixes whether the independent spatial domain is the real line,
an interval, or periodic; whether time is global or restricted; or what conclusion is asserted.

## Decisions required before statement freeze

The statement phase must identify a primary source and freeze the precise PDE (including sign and
wave-speed convention), scalar field, spatial and temporal domains, differentiability or weak
solution notion, initial and boundary data, compatibility assumptions, and the exact conclusion.
It must distinguish existence, uniqueness, representation, regularity, finite propagation, and
energy claims. It must also specify zero wave speed, zero/constant data, boundary endpoints, and
any time-orientation or characteristic-coordinate restrictions relevant to the selected theorem.

## Explicit exclusions

- Substituting the adjacent THM-M-1127 d'Alembert general-solution formula without source evidence.
- Treating the PDE itself, a definition of solution, or a candidate closed form as a theorem.
- Replacing the one-dimensional claim by an arbitrary-dimensional wave equation theorem.
- Crediting the metadata label `已验证` as a human proof or kernel receipt.
- Choosing a convenient mathlib differentiability identity before the source claim is frozen.
