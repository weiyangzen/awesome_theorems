# Scope map

## Included claim

- A real-valued function `f` on the real line, normalized by `f 0 = 1`.
- Continuity on `R`, evenness, convergence to zero at positive infinity, and convexity on
  `[0, infinity)`.
- The conclusion that `f` is the Fourier/characteristic transform of a symmetric Borel
  probability measure on `R`.

## Statement-phase decisions

The source inspection must fix whether convexity is ordinary convexity on the half-line or a
weaker midpoint condition; whether monotonicity/nonnegativity are stated or derived; and whether
the conclusion promises an absolutely continuous distribution, permits an atom, or merely gives a
probability measure. It must also fix the Fourier sign and normalization convention and distinguish
limits at `+infinity` from `|x| -> infinity` (equivalent here only after evenness).

## Explicit exclusions

- Polya's recurrence criterion for random walks, the Polya urn, or Polya frequency functions.
- Bochner's theorem alone as a substituted target.
- Assuming positive definiteness or the existence of the probability measure as a hypothesis.
- A higher-dimensional radial generalization or a necessary-and-sufficient characterization.

Degenerate and boundary cases include `x = 0`, endpoints in half-line convexity, a distribution
with a possible atom at zero, and functions whose limit is nonzero; these must be reconciled with
the selected source before formal statement credit.
