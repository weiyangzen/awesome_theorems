## 0. Mission and truthful completion boundary

Stage3 replaced the incomplete Stage2 plan during its predecessor epoch. Stage2 remains a
read-only historical input: its lossless catalog bootstrap, audit reports, Lean installation and
receipts may be reused as evidence, but its checked rows do not close any stronger Stage3 gate.

Stage3 has five required outcomes:

1. preserve and strengthen the installed Lean 4 environment, then prove current warm and isolated
   cold replay at their exact evidence boundaries;
2. convert the six-view `THM-M-0387` critique into a complete, conflict-free acceptance DAG covering
   human mathematics, Lean identity and provenance, evidence denominators, semantic validation,
   immutable receipts, benchmark tracks and independent post-repair review;
3. complete the mathematics, physics and computer-science claim lists against frozen, bounded
   coverage universes, including theorem, lemma, conjecture, hypothesis and open-problem projections,
   rather than accepting samples as a full list;
4. derive a versioned benchmark only from catalog records that pass rights, family leakage, split,
   visibility, contamination, scorer, metric and replay gates; and
5. execute every post-activation checklist item through isolated task-local Codex TUI workers under
   the current `b3ehive-execution/1.5.0` contract; the operator-authorized `S3-REL-004` bridge is not
   misrepresented as work launched by the controller that it activates.

`complete_relative_to_manifest` is the strongest permitted list-completeness claim. It means every
member of an immutable, explicitly scoped universe received a reviewed disposition and every
accepted in-scope current claim passed its applicable contract. It never means that all mathematical,
physical or computer-science knowledge has been enumerated.

Catalog completion, theorem truth, formal proof, repository integration and benchmark release are
different outcomes. An open conjecture can be a complete catalog record without being a solved proof
task. A model, device, method or dataset can be completely cataloged while remaining non-truth-apt.
`THM-M-0387` can pass dossier acceptance while `root_machine_closed=false` and
`theorem_complete=false`.

## 4. Checklist record and completion semantics

Every item is one physical line:

```text
- [STATE] `ITEM-ID` title | depends_on=ID,ID-or-- | owned_paths=path,path-or-- | gate=verifiable acceptance sentence
```

State is exactly a space, underscore or lowercase `x`. IDs are stable. Every dependency is explicit,
must exist and must form an acyclic graph. Document order is presentation only. Owned paths are exact
repository-relative POSIX paths or a deliberately owned directory root; they never include the
canonical checkout, `.git`, `.ops`, credentials or another item's path.

Worker self-test proves only its claim card. Master acceptance additionally requires applicable
repository gates on the integrated canonical tree, dependency closure, exact-path conflict review,
and reconciliation of all derived surfaces.

### 4.1 Full-list denominators

Domain completion cannot be inferred from a sample. Each domain terminal must independently prove:

1. every baseline ATO is conserved and every legacy alias resolves;
2. every provisional and newly split current sense/variant has one reviewed identity disposition;
3. every baseline occurrence and current descendant ends as an accepted exact claim or exact
   nonclaim disposition and remains conserved; only an external coverage candidate outside the
   frozen manifest scope may receive a manifest-policy exclusion, which remains counted and visible;
4. every current record has reviewed kind, atomicity, truth-aptness and applicable exact scope;
5. every theorem, lemma, conjecture, hypothesis and open problem has an exact status boundary and
   primary locator; blocked, unknown, unreviewed or deferred included members prevent completion;
6. every compound is split or has an explicit aggregate contract;
7. all accepted current truth-apt claims are `exact_structured` or `exact_formal` with provenance;
8. theorem and open-claim lists are generated queries, never hand-maintained rows; and
9. all 98 legacy review keys and every v3 audit-delta candidate receive a typed, visible disposition;
   no candidate automatically receives an ATV allocation; and
10. two clean generations are byte-identical and mutation tests reject any denominator loss.

Mathematics freezes 1,666 ATO, 1,556 mathematics-tagged provisional ATF IDs, 1,666 ATS/ATV, 1,601
aliases and 65 historically folded occurrences. Physics freezes 1,272 ATO, 1,232 domain ATF tags, 1,272 ATS/ATV, 1,263 aliases and
9 folded occurrences, plus distinct 210 source-category and 208 legacy-survivor-category sets that
must never substitute for one another. CS freezes 400 ATO, 397 domain ATF tags, 400 ATS/ATV, 398
aliases and 2 folded occurrences. Cross-domain lexical families total 66: 65 non-exact same-name
families plus the exact cross-domain Hamming family. These tags explain why domain totals do not
simply equal global 3,119 and do not establish semantic identity.

### 4.2 Open claims

Conjecture, hypothesis and open-problem status must bind exact scope, quantifiers, assumptions,
`status_as_of`, authoritative source, known special cases and resolution criterion. Historical names
such as a proved “conjecture” keep their alias but use the current status. Solved special cases never
upgrade a general open variant. Refuted claims record a witness. Conditional complexity or security
assumptions are not proved theorems. Open records are excluded from ordinary solved-proof accuracy;
they may enter only a separately governed open-challenge track.

### 4.3 Benchmark derivation

A claim catalog is not a benchmark. Source problem, canonical claim, formal-language variant, proof
asset and evaluation task are separate entities. Benchmark tasks require immutable source and
environment pins, asset-level rights, reviewed leakage components, family-safe splits, field-level
visibility, contamination labels, an executable scorer, budgets, metrics and offline replay.
Catalog totals are never benchmark denominators.

An official split name is not secrecy evidence. miniF2F v2 exposes informal proofs for test items,
and ProofNet, Lean Workbook, TheoremQA and SciBench expose answer or gold fields in some published
assets. Those bytes require explicit visibility and contamination roles. Upstream scorer behavior,
including SciBench numerical evaluation, is comparator evidence only and never silently becomes the
Stage3 scorer policy.

### 5.1 Task isolation and Codex transport

Each claim owns one root:

```text
.ops/stage3-execution-v1/tasks/<claim-id>/<run-id>/
  work/
  codex-home/
  tmux.sock
  claim.json
  result.json
```

Only exact owned paths and individually justified read-only bootstrap files are materialized with
independent inodes. Full repository copies, clones, archives, rsync/reflink/hardlink trees, mounts,
runtime state and secrets are forbidden.

The immutable claim card binds mode, claim/run/item identity, task root, baseline and card digests,
dependencies, deadline, concise deliverable, exact writable paths, justified read-only bootstrap
files, per-claim artifact allow/deny policy, validators, result schema and retry budget. The controller
recomputes both card and baseline hashes immediately before launch and again before harvest; either
boundary fails closed on drift.

Each private `CODEX_HOME` receives only the credentials and minimal route/provider configuration
needed for that claim. Project trust history, plugins, marketplaces, MCP configuration, prior
threads, goals, logs, caches and SQLite registries are forbidden bootstrap inputs.

The executable controller must freeze and test:

```text
WORKER_TRANSPORT=tmux_codex_tui
WORKER_GOAL_COMMAND=/goal
APP_SERVER_WORKERS=forbidden
CODEX_PROCESS_ISOLATION=one_process_tree_per_claim
CODEX_STATE_ISOLATION=one_writable_home_per_claim
```

Every Codex claim uses one task-local tmux server/socket/session, one ordinary interactive TUI process
tree, one private writable `CODEX_HOME`, one authenticated thread and exactly one authenticated active
`/goal` per claim. App-server, JSON-RPC multiplexing, `codex exec`, shared daemon, shared tmux, shared
writable Codex state and no-tmux Codex are hard failures without fallback.

The launch command is constructed only as argv arrays and freezes the interactive shape `codex -C
<work-root> -c features.goals=true --no-alt-screen`, with model, reasoning effort and service tier
arguments added only when operator policy explicitly selects them. It starts through task-local
`tmux -S <task-socket> -f /dev/null new-session -d -s <session> -c <work-root> env`, unsets
`CODEX_CI`, `CODEX_THREAD_ID` and `CODEX_REMOTE_PAYLOAD`, and sets only that task's private
`CODEX_HOME`; shell-evaluated command strings are rejected.

Startup is durable:

```text
reserved -> materialized -> tmux_started -> goal_pasted -> goal_submitted
         -> live -> handoff_ready -> finished
```

The controller persists the exact pane PID and `/proc` process-start identity before it sends any TUI
input. For a fresh private home it then handles only the currently active first-run or trust prompt,
then detects the real idle composer rather than a selector, modal or stale scrollback glyph. It pastes
one short `/goal` through a task-local tmux buffer, waits until a claim-specific final token is visible
in joined composer text, and submits once. It never resends a partial goal or sprays Enter. Only exact
route, tmux, PID/start time, cwd, private home, thread and active-goal identity counts as live. Healthy
delayed registration stays `starting` until the hard deadline and may be promoted without relaunch.

### 5.2 Handoff, integration, repair and cleanup

Harvest a checksum-valid per-item result and patch into immutable queue storage before stale pruning,
then stop the finished TUI immediately. The result binds actual changed paths, patch checksum,
commands and observed outcomes, artifact references and `status=self_tested`; it records only
evidence applicable to that item and cannot manufacture universal passed categories for inapplicable
gates. Each immutable queue entry also binds dependencies, conflicts, validation hints, retry class
and current state. Repair reuses the same task, thread and active goal; it never submits a second
`/goal`, and its budget is keyed by stable claim, baseline and failure identity so a restart or attempt
rename cannot reset it. Master
alone applies patches to the preserved dirty canonical checkout, resolves conflicts, runs applicable
gates and writes `[x]`. Validation failure preserves the handoff and moves it to bounded repair without
pinning unrelated work.

If any `S3-M38-060..065` review discovers a new or reopened finding, Master reopens the earliest
affected owning M38 row to `[ ]`, records the stable finding ID and invalidation closure, and routes
repair through that row's same claim, thread and goal. The former `M38-030` candidate, `M38-029`
subject digest and all six review attempts remain immutable content-addressed superseded history; a
new candidate and subject revision invalidate every old review acceptance, and all six reviewers plus
`M38-066` rerun against the replacement subject before downstream replay can resume.

The global lease covers only short state transitions. TUI startup, network, model work, builds, tests,
benchmarks and integration validation run outside it. One bounded admission pump repeatedly launches
fanout-two waves and must reach six authenticated fixture lanes in one invocation when six eligible,
conflict-free claims and host headroom exist. Every underfill records a concrete dependency, conflict,
startup, route, resource, external-limit, validator or budget reason. A lane that finishes while the
pump is still ramping counts as separately persisted completed throughput, never as launch failure or
as a missing live slot without explanation.

Each retry-safe scheduler tick preserves this phase trace: (1) acquire the repository lease and
validate the frozen specification; (2) harvest durable handoffs before pruning; (3) reconcile dead,
mismatched, interrupted, finished and accepted claims; (4) validate the Blueprint DAG and reconcile
the current projections; (5) reserve one bounded integration batch and integrate only after releasing
the lease for slow validation; (6) reacquire briefly to merge integration outcomes and reserve a
bounded dependency-ready conflict-safe claim set; (7) release the lease before materialization, TUI
startup and the bounded multiwave admission pump; and (8) reacquire briefly to merge outcomes and
atomically replace Status, Kanban and finally Gantt before the tick returns. A durable phase cursor
makes every interruption resumable, and no worker inherits the lease descriptor.

Explicit stop first harvests any valid handoff and gives every harvested handoff an explicit
integrated, rejected, or content-addressed preserved-outside-runtime disposition before removing
`.ops`. It then removes only the exact Stage3 marker and attributable controller tasks, processes,
sockets, locks and runtime, preserves canonical artifacts, and never writes a completion-success
receipt or changes a checkbox. It waits one frozen original scheduler cadence and rechecks that no
cron source recreated any owned process or runtime surface, still without writing a completion-success
receipt. Completion cleanup is a separate automatic lifecycle transition after
the final checklist row is Master accepted. It requires zero `[ ]`, zero `[_]`, every
handoff/integration/repair/checkpoint queue empty, all acceptance profiles passing and all projections
reconciled; consumes the durable `cleanup_pending` record at
`Docs/evidence/stage3_pre_cleanup.json`. That arm is schema-validated and binds its ID and SHA256,
the accepted Blueprint/specification, zero unfinished rows, empty queues and the final pre-teardown
projection snapshot; the terminal receipt must consume the exact arm rather than merely self-assert
cleanup readiness. It then performs scoped teardown; waits one original scheduler
cadence; and has an external canonical Master run the frozen side-effect-free
`scripts/stage3_execution_cleanup.py --verify-absence --format=json` command. Its script digest,
exact argv/cwd, raw stdout bytes and strict structured payload must independently crosswalk the exact
cron marker, scheduler, task processes, tmux sockets, locks and runtime root to observed absence; an
exit-zero unrelated command or self-asserted boolean is never evidence. Only then does
it write schema-validated `Docs/evidence/stage3_cleanup.json`; the projector hashes this optional
terminal input, preserves externally verified cleanup state after `.ops` is absent, and atomically
refreshes final Status, Kanban and Gantt. A missing receipt projects `null/not_started`; a present
receipt with a stale digest, invalid absence evidence or unreconciled snapshot is a hard failure. The
goal is not complete until this final receipt and projections validate.

## 6. Status, Kanban and Gantt contract

`Stage3_Status.json` separates planning-DAG readiness from observed runtime state. It reports checklist
counts, implementation/validation/integration frontiers and planning dependency blockers, then
separately reports configured and effective logical targets, admitted saturation and exact underfill
stop reasons; logical claims, reserved lanes, starting lanes, authenticated live goals, running turns,

never live.

Gantt view plus a one-to-one monitoring index with checklist state, dependencies, ownership and every

specification SHA256, runtime snapshot SHA256 or explicit absence, snapshot ID and UTC generation
time. Only recorded timestamps or operator-frozen estimates may be rendered; all current items remain
in a visible `Unscheduled` state until such evidence exists. Status and Kanban are replaced first and
Gantt last through same-directory atomic replacement after one reconciled state merge.
