## Statement and interpretation

For a finite real sequence `x`, the frozen definition collects sums over finite index sets on which `x` is monotone or antitone. A real number `c` is admissible when, after any positive loss `ε`, every sufficiently long injective sequence has one of those sums at least

`(c - ε) / sqrt(n) * sum x`.
