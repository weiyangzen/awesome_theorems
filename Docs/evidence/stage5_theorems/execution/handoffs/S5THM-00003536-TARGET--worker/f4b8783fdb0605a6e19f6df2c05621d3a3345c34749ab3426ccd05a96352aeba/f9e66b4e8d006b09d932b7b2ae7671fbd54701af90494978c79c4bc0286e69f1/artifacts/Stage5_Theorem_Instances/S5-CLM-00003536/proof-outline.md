# Proof outline

Let \(O_\xi=\{\xi 2^m3^n\pmod 1:m,n>0\}\) and let \(C_\xi\) be its closure in the unit additive circle.

1. The source and target propositions use exactly the same binders, positive-index side conditions, powers, natural-to-real cast, quotient map, set comprehension, and density predicate.
2. Multiplying an orbit point by 2 (respectively 3) increments `m` (respectively `n`), so continuity makes the closure forward invariant under both maps.
3. If the orbit were finite, two distinct 3-smooth multipliers would give the same circle point. Their nonzero integer difference times \(\xi\) would be integral, contradicting irrationality. Thus the closure is infinite.
4. Furstenberg's closed-set lemma says that an infinite closed subset of the circle invariant under both ×2 and ×3 is the whole circle. Multiplicative independence is essential here; either map alone admits proper infinite invariant closed sets.
5. Consequently \(C_\xi\) is the whole circle, which is the definition of density of \(O_\xi\).

The seven proof units split identity, orbit construction, invariance, infinitude, the closed-set argument, the closure/density conversion, and final composition so no one generic prose fragment stands for multiple logical nodes.
