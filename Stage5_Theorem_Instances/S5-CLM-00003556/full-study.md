# Full study: the Kanold diameter variant

## Statement and hypotheses

For a finite set `A` of points in the Euclidean plane, `DistancesSeparated A` says that whenever two distances determined by points of `A` differ, their absolute difference is at least one. The frozen claim asks for a positive real `C` and an eventual assertion over natural `n`: every such `A` of cardinality `n` has diameter at least `n^(3/4)`. The quantified `C` does not occur in the bound, but its positivity remains part of the exact source proposition and is not deleted.
